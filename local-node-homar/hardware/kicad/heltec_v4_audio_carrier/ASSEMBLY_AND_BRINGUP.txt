HELTEC V4.3 R2 AUDIO CARRIER - C5 ASSEMBLY NOTES

This is a CAD assembly plan. Physical fit and hardware tests remain pending.

Before fabrication
  Compare the 1:1 sheet with the owned Heltec V4.3 R2, round INMP441,
  MAX98357A, 6 x 6 x 8 mm tactile switch and axial R8 resistor. Verify pin identity, hole fit,
  body registration, underside components, acoustic path and solder access.
  Check USB/coax access and antenna/enclosure clearance. The RF reservation
  is an engineering allowance. Preview gaps are Heltec 5 mm and audio modules
  3 mm; confirm the actual assembly. Rerun CAD checks after geometry changes.

Fitted components
  U1: Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4.3 R2, USB-up.
  U2: round INMP441, acoustic/solder face up and L/R to GND.
  U3/JAMP1: MAX98357A and seven-pin interconnect.
      Pins 1..7: VIN, GND, SD_MODE, GAIN, DIN, BCLK, LRC.
  R8: EEEEE 1 kohm, 1/2 W, 5% carbon axial through-hole resistor.
      This is the single SD_MODE-to-GND pulldown; no other carrier passives.
      Form the leads to 15.24 mm pitch for horizontal mounting without
      forcing the body. The reference body envelope is 10.0 x 3.6 mm;
      carrier drills are 1.0 mm and pads are 2.0 mm.
      Check body, lead and hole fit; trim protruding leads after soldering.
  SW1: normally-open 6 x 6 x 8 mm PTT switch, FIT.
      Contact1 is GPIO39/PTT_N; contact2 is GND. Each contact has two pads
      across 6.5 mm; contacts are separated by 4.5 mm. Check the actual
      switch's permanent pairs and normally-open action before soldering.
  LS1: external speaker wired directly to amplifier SPK+ and SPK-.
      Neither output is ground. Use paired insulated wires and strain relief.
  Existing module passives remain; inspect bypassing and straps.
  TP1-TP13 are copper test pads requiring no fitted posts.

Assembly
  1. Inspect the bare board and drill registration.
  2. Fit R8 and SW1; inspect joints, resistor value and switch operation.
     R8 is non-polarized. Its 1 kohm / 5% bands are brown-black-red-gold.
  3. Fit independent mounting hardware and measured module standoffs.
  4. Align module headers without forcing them; preserve solder access.
  5. Keep solder, flux, adhesive and cleaning fluid out of the microphone port.
  6. Inspect joints, underside clearance, USB/OLED access and antenna wiring.
  7. Attach speaker wires at the amplifier terminals and add the node identity.

Power and acceptance
  Modules share Heltec USB power and power up together. Disconnect power
  before altering wiring. If a module is disconnected for troubleshooting,
  prevent GPIOs from driving it while unpowered.

  Unpowered: check shorts, polarity and every module pin/net assignment.
  Powered: verify boot, USB communication and 3V3/5V rails under load.
  Check the source, cable and Heltec power path. Amplifier 5V is VUSB before
  Heltec fuse F1; wider carrier traces do not raise upstream current limits.
  Measure amplifier SD_MODE at reset (target below 0.08 V) and during playback.
  Confirm breakout bypassing and actual speaker impedance/rating.

  PTT firmware must configure GPIO39 as an input with pullup and debounce;
  pressed reads LOW. Verify released/pressed readings before PTT actions.
  Do not drive GPIO39 as an output. Firmware remains ENABLE_PTT=false;
  the enabled path uses INPUT_PULLUP.

  With compatible audio firmware, verify microphone capture and a low-level
  speaker tone. Expected audio clocks are 8 kHz WS and nominal 512 kHz BCLK,
  with 32-bit stereo wire slots and 16-bit mono stored PCM. Verify audible
  playback directly. Connect probe ground only to GND, never a speaker output.
  Run audio/radio integration tests with compatible firmware and verified
  simultaneous supply capacity. Preserve both matched antennas.

Record CAD checks, physical fit, power measurements, audio and RF tests separately.
