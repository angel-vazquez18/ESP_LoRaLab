HELTEC V4 R2 AUDIO CARRIER - C5
KiCad 10.0.8 | 2026-11-24

Open heltec_v4_audio_carrier.kicad_pro in KiCad.
The 70 x 90 mm four-layer carrier uses project-local symbols, footprints and
models. This folder contains the current design and supporting references.

ASSEMBLY
U1: Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4.3 R2, USB-up (J2 left, J3 right).
U2: round INMP441 module; acoustic/solder face up, L/R grounded.
U3: MAX98357A module on the JAMP1 seven-pin interconnect; GAIN grounded.
R8: EEEEE 1 kohm, 1/2 W, 5% carbon axial through-hole pulldown.
It is the only carrier passive; mount horizontally and solder both leads.
SW1: fitted 6 x 6 x 8 mm normally-open PTT switch, GPIO39/PTT_N to GND.
LS1: external speaker wired directly to amplifier SPK+ and SPK-.
Neither speaker terminal is GND. TP1-TP13 are exposed copper test pads.
Signal and power paths are direct copper. The carrier has no capacitors or
jumper resistors; it uses the existing breakout-module circuitry.

PTT
The reference footprint uses a 6.5 x 4.5 mm contact grid, 2 mm pads and 1.1 mm
holes. The upper pair is contact1/PTT_N; the lower pair is contact2/GND.
Verify actual switch pairing and fit. Pressed reads LOW with an input pullup.
Firmware remains ENABLE_PTT=false; the enabled path uses INPUT_PULLUP.

VALIDATION
Current CAD checks and route data are in reports/. Physical fit and hardware
operation have not been tested. Compare the 1:1 sheet with the owned parts
before fabrication. Check pin identity, holes, header registration, underside
clearance, acoustic opening, USB/coax access and antenna/enclosure clearance.
The RF reservation is an engineering allowance. Preview gaps are 5 mm for
Heltec and 3 mm for audio modules. USB supply capacity, module bypassing and
audible playback need bench validation. AMP_DATA exceeds the preferred 50 mm
route length because of its RF-reservation detour.
