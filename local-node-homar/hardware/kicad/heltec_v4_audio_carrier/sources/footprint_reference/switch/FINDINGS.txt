PTT SWITCH REFERENCE - CURRENT C5

SW1 is the selected OCR TPBS02 / Amazon B01MRP025V 6 x 6 x 8 mm
normally-open tactile switch. It is fitted in the design.

The adopted reference grid is 6.5 x 4.5 mm, from KiCad SW_PUSH_6mm and
the C&K PTS645 straight THT drawing. This does not identify the owned OCR
switch as a C&K part. Verify its actual fit and contact pairing.

Centered native footprint coordinates (X right, Y down):
  pad1 / PTT_N: (-3.25,-2.25), (+3.25,-2.25)
  pad2 / GND:   (-3.25,+2.25), (+3.25,+2.25)
Each 6.5 mm pair is permanently common; pressing joins the two groups.
The carrier joins each pair in copper. GPIO39 connects directly to PTT_N.

Carrier pads are 2 mm with 1.1 mm drills, matching the KiCad reference.
The manufacturer reference shows 1.0 mm holes; the owned lead fit is untested.
The representative 8 mm-high KiCad STEP is fitted at (-3.25,+2.25,0) mm
to recenter its original pad-based origin on the carrier footprint.

Firmware is unchanged: ENABLE_PTT=false. Its enabled branch uses GPIO39
INPUT_PULLUP. Check active-low behavior and debounce when enabling it.

Preserved evidence: installed-SW_PUSH_6mm-reference.kicad_mod,
manufacturer-pts645-conrad-mirror.pdf, pts645-page2-reference.png.
