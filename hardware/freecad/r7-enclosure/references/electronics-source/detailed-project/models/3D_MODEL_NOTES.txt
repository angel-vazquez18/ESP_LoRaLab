Active model attachments are listed in detailed-models.json. The assembly
shows U1, U2, U3/JAMP1, fitted SW1 and R8. The speaker is external and wires
directly to the amplifier terminal. The carrier has no other passive parts.

HELTEC V4 R2
The original colored manufacturer STEP contains the board, connectors,
header pins and electronic components:
https://resource.heltec.cn/download/WiFi_LoRa_32_V4/3D/HTIT-WB32LAF_V4.3.1%281%29.step
All 36 reference header axes register with the carrier. Attachment: scale 1,
rotation Z +90 degrees, offset (0,23.749,6.55871926) mm.

The STEP lacks the OLED assembly. The separate visual supplement adds the
dark screen, bracket, supports and screw heads using the manufacturer's
drawing and R2 product photographs:
https://heltec.org/project/wifi-lora-32-v4-r2
Attachment: scale 1, offset/rotation 0. The screen is shown unpowered.

The preview places the PCB underside 5 mm above the carrier. Reference CAD
gives about 1.40 mm underside-connector clearance and header tails extending
about 1.90 mm below the 1.6 mm carrier. Header registration places the body
about 0.83 mm from the provisional 2D outline center longitudinally.
These are reference-CAD calculations, not owned-unit measurements.

MAX98357A

pads, packages, markings and green speaker terminal. Body dimensions and
2.54 mm header pitch follow the user drawing. Component heights, terminal
shape, minor placement, thickness and solder details are visual estimates.
PCB underside gap: 3 mm. VRML unit: 2.54 mm; scale 1, offset/rotation 0.
See MAX98357A_PURPLE_DETAILED_VISUAL.notes.json.

INMP441

Contact grid: 2.54 x 7.62 mm. PCB underside gap: 3 mm. Visual coordinates
use millimeters; KiCad scale is 1/2.54, offset/rotation 0. Thickness, port,

PTT SWITCH
Project-local SW_PUSH_6mm_H8mm.step is the installed KiCad standard model.
It represents a 6 x 6 x 8 mm tactile with a 6.5 x 4.5 mm pin grid. Offset
(-3.25,+2.25,0) mm recenters the library origin on the carrier footprint.
It is a representative standard model, not an OCR manufacturer model.

AXIAL RESISTOR
R8 represents the user-selected EEEEE 1 kohm, 1/2 W, 5% carbon resistor
in a horizontal through-hole mounting. The model is representative; verify
the owned resistor body, lead diameter and formed pitch before assembly.
Project-local model: R_Axial_Carbon_1k_0p5W_P15p24.wrl, authored reference
geometry with brown-black-red-gold bands. Body envelope: 10.0 x 3.6 mm;
formed lead pitch: 15.24 mm. Carrier drills are 1.0 mm with 2.0 mm pads.
This is not an EEEEE manufacturer model or a measurement of the owned part.
