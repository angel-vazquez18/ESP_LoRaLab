2026-11-24 | Read-only project and manufacturer-reference review

Accepted scope
The user selected a compartment and a direct Heltec battery connection only.
The C5 amplifier power circuit, PCB, firmware and USB power path are unchanged.
The compartment must not be described as enabling a fully battery-operated
walkie-talkie: speaker playback still requires the existing USB supply.

Battery identity and mechanical input
The user identifies a Meshnology 3.7 V, 3000 mAh, model 103665 LiPo pack.
The supplied dimensional image explicitly shows a 68 x 35 x 10 mm body and
66 mm battery lead. The 530 mm dimension belongs to the separate USB charging
cable, which is not stored inside the enclosure. No manufacturing tolerance,
growth allowance or strain-relief dimensions are supplied by that image.
The model code is not a substitute for the pictured finished-pack dimensions.
A 36 mm conservative body width is an engineering envelope allowance, not a
claim that the pictured pack measures 36 mm. Verify the owned wrapped pack,
protection-board end and actual connector before production.

Manufacturer battery evidence
Meshnology's current product page describes a 3.7 V, 3000 mAh soft-pack battery,
a Micro JST 1.25 mm connector, built-in overcharge/over-discharge/short-circuit
protection, and a separate included USB charging cable rated 300 mA. It offers
variants for N32/N33/N35/N39 and another N37 variant. The page does not provide
a full cell datasheet or maximum continuous charge/discharge current for the
owned 103665 pack. Do not treat the included cable's 300 mA output as the cell's
maximum allowed charging current. Do not substitute specifications from an
unrelated 103665 manufacturer. The exact keyed connector family/part number
and pack-end pin numbering are not established by the marketing name alone.
Source [1]. The manufacturer's N39 documentation separately confirms a
Heltec V4 product supplied with a 3000 mAh battery, supporting capacity-level
compatibility but not proving the user's particular pack polarity [4].

Heltec V4.3.1 connection
Use the lithium-battery port, JP2 in the V4.3 schematic, not the nearby solar
connector JP3. Heltec calls its battery interface SH1.25-2P in the project
datasheet. Its specified battery range is 3.3 to 4.2 V, typical 3.7 V.
The diagram establishes JP2 pin 1 = VBAT positive and pin 2 = GND.
Connect the battery positive lead to VBAT and negative lead to GND. Identify
actual pin orientation from the board markings/schematic and verify voltage
and polarity at the mating plug before connection; matching pitch and red/
black wire colors alone are insufficient to establish pin assignment.
If a harness extension is needed, use a matching polarized two-pin 1.25 mm
pair and verify end-to-end polarity. No adapter length is qualified by this
electrical review; the measured cable route controls the required length.
Sources [2], [3].

Charging
Heltec's datasheet lists 500 mA charging with 5 V USB. The V4.3 schematic uses
a CN3165 and annotates a nominal constant-current setting of 540 mA with
R13 = 2.2 kohm. Confirm the exact pack permits that charging current before
using Heltec USB charging; this review found no primary pack current rating.
The manufacturer's included external charging lead is an alternative for a
disconnected pack. Do not parallel it with the Heltec charger on the same
connected battery. The two-wire pack has no separate temperature-sense lead.
The drawing does not establish cell-temperature supervision. Sources [1]-[3].

Existing C5 power circuit - exact project evidence
The current KiCad netlist has a direct /+5V_AMP net containing only:
  U1 J2_02 (5V_USB), JAMP1 pin 1 (VIN), U3 VIN, and TP3 pin 1.
There is no carrier fuse, diode OR, boost converter, battery switch or
alternative supply on that net. The Heltec schematic ties this exported
header pin to VUSB, ahead of F1, and does not boost the battery to that rail.
The battery can supply the Heltec and its regulated 3V3 microphone rail;
PTT is a GPIO-to-ground switch and is not a separate powered module.
Battery-only operation does not power the amplifier through its current VIN
connection. Do not jumper VBAT onto /+5V_AMP: that would join the pack to the
USB VBUS path rather than implement a managed dual-source supply.

Battery-only signal constraint
The /AMP_SD net directly joins Heltec GPIO48 (U1 J2_14) to JAMP1 pin 3 and
U3 SD_MODE, with R8 providing a 1 kohm pull-down. Analog Devices specifies
SD_MODE within -0.3 V to VDD + 0.3 V. With USB absent, the amplifier VIN/VDD
is unpowered, so actively driving GPIO48 HIGH exceeds this input rating and
may back-power the amplifier. The pull-down does not protect an input driven
HIGH by the ESP32. Hold AMP_SD LOW whenever the amplifier supply is absent;
merely stopping audio samples does not establish that this requirement is met.
The same datasheet explicitly permits LRCLK/BCLK/DIN up to 6 V independently
of VDD, so the SD_MODE restriction should not be incorrectly generalized to
those three audio inputs. Source [5], p4 Absolute Maximum Ratings.
The normal C5-compatible audio firmware has a specific battery-only startup
conflict, detailed below. Firmware changes and operating tests remain outside
this mechanical revision.

Searching src, lib and include found no VBUS/VUSB, USB-present/power or
battery-supply detection path gating amplifier enable.

Conclusion: assuming normal I2S initialization succeeds, this ordinary Heltec
audio build drives GPIO48 HIGH on a battery-only boot even without an audio
command. With the retained USB-only amplifier supply absent, that exceeds
the SD_MODE input rating described above. It also leaves SD HIGH if USB is
removed after a successful USB-powered startup. The standard audio image is
not suitable for battery-only commissioning with this unchanged carrier.

Required commissioning prerequisite, not performed by this revision:
Use a verified Heltec-profile build with AUDIO_ENABLE_SPEAKER=0 (or a later
properly qualified supply-aware implementation) before connecting the pack
for operation without USB. Source inspection shows that with the flag zero,
begin still executes outputLow(SPK_SD) at line177 and short-circuits the
configureSpeakerI2S call at line179. outputLow at lines36-43 disconnects the
GPIO-matrix route, selects a pull-down, preloads LOW and sets output mode.
Therefore the expected software behavior is an actively driven LOW on
GPIO48 after successful GPIO initialization; this is stronger than relying
only on the carrier's 1 kohm pull-down. GPIO startup/transition behavior and
the actual SD_MODE voltage still require bench measurement. The existing
mic_only environment inherits esp32dev and uses the legacy pin profile;
do not select it unchanged for the Heltec. Preserve the Heltec board/profile
and add the speaker-disable setting in a separately authorized firmware task.
Do not merely mute playback or unplug the speaker: neither removes the
electrical SD_MODE drive into the unpowered amplifier.
No firmware edit, build, flash, reset, serial-port access or battery test was
performed. Source analysis is not a claim about an installed image or a
measured waveform. Before battery-only testing, verify the installed image
and measure the shutdown input LOW through boot and USB removal.

Reference connector geometry, not an approved replacement part
Molex 532610271 and mating 510210200 are useful PicoBlade 1.25 mm references.
The header's 7.65 x approximately 5.2 x 3.4 mm envelope matches the nominal
STEP envelope, but dimensional agreement alone does not establish the exact
installed manufacturer/part or prove the supplied pack's connector mates.
The 510210200 drawing gives a 4.25 mm width and 3.95 mm housing depth;
the 532610271 drawing sheet 2 contains a mated view. Its 5.2 mm reference
dimension must not be treated as 5.2 mm of plug projecting outside the socket.
The exact dimension-arrow endpoints were not visually qualified in this
electrical review, so no derived external protrusion is released. Sources
[6] and [7]. Hardware fit and polarity verification still control selection.

Mechanical integration implications
Provide a smooth isolated pocket, separation from exposed solder/header pins,
retention without rigid compression of the pouch, accessible cable strain
relief and room to unplug the battery for service. Leave dimensional margin
around the wrapped/protected pack. The enclosure clearance is not a certified
swelling, thermal, impact or cell-safety qualification. Do not reserve space
for an unspecified boost converter: the user explicitly retained the original
USB-powered amplifier circuit for this revision.
