C5 R7 - ENCLOSURE IMPROVEMENTS
2026-11-25

This revision implements enclosure-only changes after the R6 design review.
The PCB is already in fabrication. Its geometry, component placement, mounting
datums, Gerbers, drills and firmware remain the existing baseline. The integrity
guard checks 367 protected files for both byte and membership changes.

CHANGES
- Reinforced speaker retainer: nut-bearing roof increased from 0.8 to 1.4 mm;
  retainer thickness increased from 2.6 to 3.2 mm. Speaker position and the
  original hard-stop plane are retained.
- Local OLED short-edge bands and corner returns strengthen the sampled thin
  roof regions while preserving the 0.50 mm nominal glass-to-rim gap, aperture,
  display position and main long-edge outer face.
- Noncontact microphone shroud directs the existing acoustic path. The modeled
  minimum clearance to the actual assigned microphone features is 0.70 mm.
- Separate loose cable-tie guides retain the speaker pair and coax position.
  Conservative tie-head references and local lip reliefs were checked.
- Removable SMA anti-rotation key fits a guided pocket and is retained by the
  closed lid. The existing round bulkhead bore and seating wall are preserved.
- R10 reference coax route provides a practical routing target with a 132.165 mm
  centerline. Actual cable length, slack and bend limits must be checked.
- Optional fit coupon supports first-article insert, nut and plunger checks.

CONFIGURATION
Seven installed printed parts: base, lid, battery cover, PTT plunger, PTT stop
cup, speaker retainer and SMA key. The optional fit coupon is an eighth part and
is not installed. The battery cover, PTT plunger and PTT cup geometry is retained
from R6. No electrical redesign is included.

FILES AND USE
cad/ contains the native parametric FCStd and neutral STEP after local approval.
printing/ contains the approved multipart 3MF master and individual STL/3MF files.
craftcloud/upload/ contains one part per file, translated to a nonnegative origin.
craftcloud/optional-coupon/ keeps the coupon separate from the seven-part order.
documentation/ contains the dimensioned change drawing, hardware BOM, assembly
sequence and first-article inspection record.
interactive/ contains the assembled, exploded and cutaway inspection viewer.
audit/ contains geometry, unchanged-PCB and file-validation evidence.

Upload only one format per part, not both STL and 3MF, at 100% scale in mm.
The combined master and STEP include the offset coupon; do not submit them as a
single fused print. The Craftcloud directory is the intended upload set.
No order, replacement PCB release, machine G-code or firmware update is performed
by this enclosure revision. The user separately authorized publishing the
validated FreeCAD development archive and project-adoption documentation.

LIMITS TO RESOLVE ON THE FIRST ARTICLE
The SMA reference rear body is an unmeasured 8 mm across-flats assumption and
the nominal key gap is 8.4 mm. Measure the owned fixed rear body and adjust the
parametric key if necessary; a mounting-nut measurement is not equivalent.
The 0.25 mm PTT switch depression remains provisional until the actual switch is
measured. Inspect the owned OLED stack, battery/USB plug combination, speaker
frame and service slack before closing the enclosure. The detailed inspection
record identifies these interfaces. Geometry checks do not establish impact,
torque, ingress, thermal or acoustic performance ratings.

The amplifier remains USB powered. The previously recorded battery-only
enable/firmware commissioning issue is outside this enclosure-only revision.

RELEASE STATUS
The package builder refuses to produce the handoff ZIP until approved native
exports, exact source comparisons, print-file validation, documentation review,
saved-native viewer checks and the protected-file guard all pass. A draft viewer
or candidate BREP alone is not a released manufacturing file.
