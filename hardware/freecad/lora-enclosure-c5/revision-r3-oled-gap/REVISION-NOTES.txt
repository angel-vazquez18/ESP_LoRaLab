LoRa C5 enclosure - R3 nominal 0.50 mm OLED window gap
2026-11-24 | Units: mm | Prototype revision

R3 lowers only the local OLED bezel by 0.50 mm. The main lid, carrier PCB,
Heltec module, PTT mechanism and all other mounting datums retain their R2
positions. Four radius 2.2 mm underside reliefs preserve clearance over the
OLED screw details. A full-lid drop or an unrelieved lowered rim would reduce
the modeled screw clearance to about 0.20 mm and was not used.

Nominal fit (modeled glass top Z19.998719)
Local window underside:             Z20.500000
Recessed local exterior face:       Z22.100000
Main lid underside:                Z21.000000 (unchanged)
Main lid exterior face:            Z24.600000 (unchanged)
Glass to local underside plane:     0.501281, shown as 0.50 mm
Glass to local exterior face:       2.101281, shown as 2.10 mm
Minimum OLED frame clearance:       0.441281
Minimum OLED screw clearance:       0.698781
Normal local rim thickness:         1.60
Minimum roof over screw reliefs:    1.10

The display aperture remains open. The 0.50 mm dimension is a vertical
reference gap to the underside of the surrounding rim, not a distance to a
solid ceiling above the glass. No lens, gasket or display compression is added.

Geometry
Window opening: 16.4 x 27, centered X23, Y58.05.
Recess floor: 20.4 x 31 at Z22.1; 2 mm-wide ledge around the opening.
Recess at main exterior face: 25.4 x 36, with 45-degree transitions.
Screw relief centers: X16 and X30; Y43.55 and Y72.55; radius 2.2.
Reliefs remove only the newly lowered material, ending at original Z21.
The window cutter extends through the lowered rim to keep the aperture open.

Parametric controls
B73 = 1.6 normal local rim thickness; B74 = 2.0 ledge width.
B78 = 0.5 local bezel drop; B79 = B44 - B78 (local underside).
B75 = B9 - B73 + B78 (recess depth); B76 = B79 + B73 (local face).
B80 = 2.2 head relief radius; B81 = 14 and B82 = 29 screw-center spans.
OLEDLowerRim is a native Part box fused into LidWithClamps.
OLEDHeadRelief1..4 are native cylinders linked into LidCutters.
The existing native ruled loft drives the 45-degree exterior recess.

Files and use
cad/LoRa-Enclosure-C5-R3-OLED.FCStd is the editable native revision.
The matching STEP contains all four printable parts in assembled poses.
printing/ contains the revised lid STL and 3MF in millimetres; position and
orient for a build plate in a slicer if printing locally.
The base, PTT plunger and stop cup remain unchanged from R1/R2. Replace the
previous lid with this R3 lid when preparing a prototype order. Previous
R1/R2 order ZIPs and drawings remain historical and do not contain R3.
documentation/ contains the updated dimensioned section from saved geometry.
audit/ contains native/clearance and mesh/export verification evidence.

Prototype limitations
The glass, bracket and screw geometry are supplemental approximations, and
the module mounting height has not been measured on the owned hardware.
A 0.50 mm nominal gap has less tolerance margin than R2. Verify the prototype
against the actual OLED, headers and fastening hardware before production.
The 1.10 mm local relief roofs are intended for the established MJF PA12
prototype process, not a verified impact/structural rating. Supplier process
capability and dimensional variation must be considered in physical fit.
No electronics, firmware, PCB fabrication or wiring changes are included.
