R7 enclosure inspection viewer

The viewer is a derived inspection aid. Printing and machining use the released
R7 CAD and manufacturing files, not its capped display cuts or exploded offsets.

The populated C5 PCB and all 2,949 assigned source objects retain their existing
placements and overview mesh coordinates. The battery, speaker shape, fasteners,
SMA hardware, wires and ties remain fit references, with measured-hardware checks
listed in the R7 assembly/inspection instructions.

R7 view changes: reinforced lid relief edges, noncontact microphone shroud,
thicker speaker-retainer nut roofs, speaker lead retention, removable SMA key,
and a 10 mm nominal bend-radius coax route. The 0.50 mm OLED datum gap, PCB
positions, battery bay and speaker electrical connection are retained.

Controls include assembled/exploded/cutaway modes, rotate/zoom, shell transparency,
individual part selection/visibility, nine clickable fit dimensions, and focused
speaker/SMA/coax/microphone inspections. Exploded cable positions are illustrative
disconnections; they do not imply that the installed cables stretch.

Source preparation checks saved native solids against audited candidates and
hashes every input. Mesh verification decodes the packed data and compares all
coordinates and triangle indices. No geometric simplification is applied. The
display meshes use 0.001 mm coordinate quantization. Local clearance views use
exact Boolean sections with 18 endpoint-to-surface witnesses.

Rebuild using the installed FreeCAD Python on prepare_inputs.py, then the two
mesh exporters, build_viewer.py and browser QA; rebuild_viewer.py orchestrates
the sequence. --preflight uses candidate geometry and visibly marks a draft.
Final SOURCE-INPUTS.json and BUILD-VALIDATION.json must state saved R7 native
geometry, and qa/R7-BROWSER-VALIDATION.json must match the fragment SHA-256.
The browser report also records the tested standalone preview SHA-256 and
verifies its decoded embedded content contains the exact current fragment.
