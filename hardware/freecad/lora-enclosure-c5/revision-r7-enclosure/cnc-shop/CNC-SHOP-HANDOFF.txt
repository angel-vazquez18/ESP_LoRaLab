Coordinates are assembly coordinates, in mm. XY origin is the carrier lower-

MATERIAL AND MANUFACTURING ROUTE
Primary route is unfilled MJF PA12 printing with a standard cleaned finish.
CNC is an alternative feasibility/quotation route. The shop must propose an
insulating engineering-plastic stock and demonstrate that its behavior at the
thin sections, inserts and sliding interfaces is suitable. A billet material
is not automatically equivalent to printed PA12. Obtain agreement before any
material substitution, geometry change or insert-method change.

PROPOSED SETUP CONCEPTS - SHOP MUST DEVELOP CAM
Base: support the retained 2.4-thick PCB/battery partition. Opposed PCB and
battery cavities require re-registration; side holes, rear hood/chase, SMA
carrier and enclosed tie slots need a specific tool-access review. Some of
these additive features may be impractical to machine monolithically.
Lid: machine outer recesses/holes and underside clamps/shroud/pockets/lip in
separate supported setups. Preserve the OLED rim plane and speaker hard stops.
The acoustic shroud, tie anchor, narrow lip relief and screw-roof returns need
small-tool and workholding review; no silent removal is permitted.
Battery cover: machine guide features, flip for strap channels, and preserve
the 1.4 residual floor under the 1-deep channels. Keep strap edges smooth.
PTT plunger: use a suitable lathe/mill or small-part fixture for shaft, flange
and foot. Preserve both stop and actuator planes and diameter 8 sliding shaft.
PTT stop cup: machine cavity, through bore, shelf, lugs and nut-bearing faces.
Keep the 2-thick stop shelf and existing contact plane.
Speaker retainer: machine the frame/window and 4.3 AF nut pockets, preserving
the new 1.4 bearing roof and 3.2 overall thickness. Hex-pocket corner access
requires a shop solution that does not change seating or component clearance.
SMA key: support the 2-thick U profile and 1.1 top bridge. Trial-fit actual rear
fixed hex before batch production; 8.4 nominal jaw gap is provisional.

TOLERANCES AND DESIGN REVIEW BEFORE MACHINING
All dimensions are nominal; no blanket tolerance or tightening torque is
released. Agree supplier capability for insert pilots, locating lips, nut
pockets, screw seats, the 0.50 OLED gap and PTT travel before a production run.
Do not add fillets, dogbones, draft, extra screws or access holes without an
enclosure design review. The PCB and its mounting datums are frozen.
Finish battery-facing and cable-contact edges without removing fitted datums.
Confirm cutter reach, tool diameter, collision clearance, thin-section support,
stock clamping, release sequence and access to all internal pockets and holes.
The existing 0.25 PTT switch depression is a reference assumption; qualify
actual actuation and allowable overtravel before using a forceful stop test.

REQUIRED SHOP DELIVERABLES BEFORE MACHINE OPERATION
1. Chosen material/stock and any proposed manufacturability changes.
2. Agreed drawing tolerances, tool-access review and setup/fixture sheets.
3. Tool list, work offsets, cutting conditions and controller/postprocessor.
4. CAM simulation, collision/stock-rest checks and machine-specific program.
5. First-article inspection against R7-ASSEMBLY-AND-INSPECTION.txt.

No machine-executable G-code, spindle commands, feeds, offsets or controller
selection are supplied. The STEP is the machining geometry handoff. Gerber and
Excellon files are separately preserved PCB-fabrication inputs and are not
enclosure milling paths. No order or machining operation has been performed.

ASSEMBLY ACCEPTANCE
Check owned SMA rear body/thread engagement, actual cable/tie envelopes,
speaker rigid frame and pads, OLED/header height, PTT switch, battery/USB plug
coexistence and full service slack. The amplifier stays USB powered. Read the
preserved battery electrical commissioning review before operation.
Nominal solid-clearance checks do not establish physical load, impact, ingress,
acoustic or thermal ratings. Qualify a first article before ordering a batch.
