as a replacement PCB release. CAD calculations do not qualify physical hardware.

R7 ENCLOSURE CHANGES
1. SMA retention: a replaceable U-shaped key captures the fixed rear hex body.
   Key 14 wide x 2 axial x 9.9 high; jaw gap 8.4; top bridge 1.1; guide allowance
   0.3 per side and axially; key top Z20.3 has 0.7 to the closed lid at Z21.
   The reference rear hex is 8 AF and is unmeasured. Measure the OWNED fixed rear
   body, not the mounting nut, and adjust the parametric key gap if necessary.
   The nominal reference contacts the key at +/-8 degrees including guide play.
   This is geometric anti-rotation evidence, not a torque or antenna load rating.
2. Coax support: integrated anchor accepts a loose nominal 2.5-wide tie. The
   reference route is 132.165 long, 1.32 diameter and uses R10 bends. These are
   routing assumptions, not the owned cable specification. Reference minimum
   distances: base 0.640, lid 0.485, assigned components 0.321 near the U.FL end.
   Verify actual lead length, bend limit and full surplus slack. The tie must
   retain position without crushing the coax or pulling on the U.FL socket.
3. Speaker retainer: 3.2 thick, underside Z13.2 and original hard-stop plane
   Z16.4. The 4.3 AF nut pockets remain 1.8 deep and now leave a 1.4 bearing roof
   (was 0.8). Nuts occupy Z13.4-15.0. Existing M2x12 screws have nominal tips at
   Z12.9, projecting 0.5 beyond the nut. Speaker, pads and stop plane stay fixed.
   A separate lid anchor carries a second loose tie for the speaker lead pair.
   Both ties now use the same conservative 4.5 x 4.5 x 2.7 head envelope. The
   speaker head occupies X66.8-71.3, Y54.05-58.55, Z13.6-16.3. A local lip relief
   at X70.1-72.2, Y54.55-58.05, Z19.25-19.8 provides 0.45 loop-to-lip clearance
   and retains 1.2 lip thickness. Reference minimum clearances are 0.15 at the
   anchor slot, 0.25 to the speaker lead, 0.70 to base and 3.669 to electronics.
4. Microphone path: integrated noncontact shroud OD11, ID8, bottom Z13 and lid
   underside Z21, giving 8 protrusion and 1.5 nominal wall. Minimum assigned-
   component clearance is 0.70. No acoustic seal or performance rating is implied.
   Any optional soft gasket must fit the real stationary port/frame without
   loading the microphone or obscuring an acoustic opening.
5. OLED support: two external short-edge bands and four corner returns increase
   sampled screw-relief roof thickness from 1.1 to 1.5-1.7. Exact vertical probes
   checked 252 locations, including 196 former 1.1-thick positions; this is not
   a global minimum-wall solution. Added features stay outside the aperture.
   Main long-edge outer face Z22.1, lower rim Z20.5, OLED location and the nominal
   0.50 glass-to-rim gap are retained. Top-edge fillets R0.2 reduce sharp edges.

ASSEMBLY SEQUENCE
1. Clean and inspect printed parts. Remove loose powder, support remnants and
   sharp burrs without changing fitted datums. Confirm all holes are clear.
2. Test insert/nut/guide fits on the optional coupon with the purchased hardware.
   Install inserts using the supplier's process; support the boss and prevent
   distortion. A good coupon is useful process evidence, not proof every boss
   is acceptable. Inspect the enclosure's actual bosses after installation.
3. Fit the SMA bulkhead with its supplied mounting hardware. Trial-fit the key
   around the fixed rear hex and into its carrier. Counter-hold and tighten only
   enough to seat hardware securely; check antenna mating and wrench access.
4. Install the unchanged PCB on its original supports. Connect the correct LoRa
   U.FL socket and route coax through the loose tie. Fit all lead slack, keep
   strain off the connector and confirm the actual cable's minimum bend radius.
5. Fit the PTT assembly with its existing cup, two M2x10 screws and nuts. Verify
   free sliding and actual switch actuation/release before applying hard load.
6. Fit the speaker on the stationary-frame pads. Seat the reinforced retainer
   fully on both hard stops using its two M2x12 screws, washers and captive nuts.
   Do not trap its diaphragm, vents or wires. Remove the speaker connector as
   previously specified and clamp the leads in marked SPK+ and SPK- terminals.
   Neither speaker conductor connects to system ground. Leave service slack and
   retain the pair with the lid tie without crushing insulation.
7. Fit the padded battery and soft straps in the separate compartment. Connect
   only to the existing Heltec battery input using the preserved connection and
   polarity checks. This revision does not provide battery power to the amplifier.
8. Lower the lid slowly while watching both lead routes and microphone shroud.
   Confirm no pinch, plug interference or preload on glass/switch/PCB. Fit the
   main screws and rear cover. Recheck movement and clearances after closure.

PRINT PROCESS AND OPTIONAL COUPON
Unfilled MJF PA12, standard cleaned/depowdered finish; 100% scale in mm. Submit
one mesh format per part and distinguish the optional coupon from installed
parts. The shop chooses build orientation. Do not polish or coat critical fits
without reviewing material removal/deposition and the small bearing features.
Provider-specific tolerances govern; CAD nominal gaps are not print tolerances.

The 50 x 32 coupon has a 3.2-thick plate, two diameter 7 insert bosses with
diameter 4.4 and 4.5 pilots (5 deep, 1 floor), diameter 8.8 plunger guide (3.6
local thickness), and M2 nut pockets 4.2/4.3/4.4 AF (1.8 depth, 1.4 roof,
diameter 2.4 through). External plate edges R0.5. Pilot 4.4 is the current
basis; 4.5 is an optional comparison,
not an automatically accepted replacement. Test actual nuts and plunger. Do
not print 0.25-thick shims and assume they qualify the PTT operating travel.

FUNCTIONAL QUALIFICATION AFTER FIT
Record repeated PTT and closure cycles, antenna fitting/removal, rear-cover
servicing, closed-case speaker/microphone performance and temperatures at the
intended duty. Initial handling/drop development uses inert battery mass.
Define acceptance loads and environments before claiming strength. No weather,
IP, impact, acoustic or thermal rating is established by the model or drawings.
