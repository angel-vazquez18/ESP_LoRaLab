WHY THIS MATERIAL
MJF PA12 is the primary engineering recommendation for the handheld enclosure
and its small mechanism: powder-bed support avoids removal scars, and the
material offers useful impact/heat resistance and similar strength across
build directions. Craftcloud lists 0.08 mm nominal layers and 0.8 mm minimum
walls. These catalog values are not guarantees for this particular geometry.
The actual quotation must confirm the chosen shop's process and tolerances.
Source: https://craftcloud3d.com/en/material-guide/hp-mjf-nylon-pa12

ALTERNATIVES FOR COMPARISON
SLS unfilled PA12 is an acceptable alternative for quotation. It is a tough
engineering plastic with a matte/grainy surface and no sacrificial supports.
Craftcloud lists 0.1 mm layers and 0.8 mm minimum walls. Select it if the shop
offers a better confirmed fit/finish plan or delivered price for this set.
Source: https://craftcloud3d.com/en/material-guide/sls-nylon-pa12

PETG FDM is a useful comparison for a functional fit prototype. Craftcloud
lists good impact resistance, 0.2 mm layers, 1 mm minimum walls and 20% default
infill. If chosen, request the engineering print settings and support plan
in the final printing instructions, rather than accepting default 20% infill
for the small loaded plunger/cup. Confirm underside surface finish, small
acoustic holes, screw holes and support removal at the quoted shop. This
option needs more attention to build direction and sliding-surface cleanup.
Source: https://craftcloud3d.com/en/material-guide/petg

CRITICAL FIT REVIEW BEFORE PRODUCTION
The nominal design has a 0.25 mm switch depression to the stop, a 0.40 mm radial
plunger-guide clearance and 0.40 mm per-side locating-lip clearance. These are
CAD nominal values, not demonstrated manufacturing capability or switch limits.

Craftcloud publishes the same general accuracy for MJF and SLS PA12:
+/-0.3% with a lower limit of +/-0.3 mm. PETG is listed as +/-0.3%, with a
lower limit of +/-0.2 mm. Those tolerances are too broad to guarantee this
mechanism's nominal stroke and mating fits without additional control.
For example, an 8.8 mm guide and 8.0 mm shaft, each at +/-0.3 mm, can retain
only 0.2 mm diametral clearance in the worst opposing size condition; surface
roughness and shape error can reduce the useful sliding clearance further.

Have the selected manufacturer review the final dimensioned drawings and
state achievable tolerances or a measured finishing plan for:
1. Guide bore and plunger shaft: free sliding after finishing.
2. Flange-to-stop-shelf axial relation and cup-to-lid seating: retain the
   specified geometry and document the actual dimensions; do not simply sand
   the stop surfaces until the button moves.
3. Locating lip and enclosure seat: lid closes without forcing or bowing.
4. Display bezel and PCB support datums: retain the final designed screen gap.
   The enclosure must not clamp the display glass.
5. Microphone openings: all holes open, clean and free of powder or support.
6. M3 insert seats and M2 fastener holes: match ruthex RX-M3x5x4 inserts (5.0 mm
   outside diameter, 4.0 mm length), with nominal 4.4 mm pilots 5.0 mm deep,
   and the final drawing. Manufacturer minimum surrounding wall is 1.3 mm.
   Hardware installation is not part of this four-print order
   unless a separate service and exact insert specification are agreed.

Insert source: https://www.igo3d.com/mediafiles/Sonstiges/Ruthex/ruthex_Datenblatt_RX-Serie.pdf

The actual installed PTT switch has not been identified by a measured travel
specification. The comparable-switch 0.25 mm value remains provisional.
Vendor dimensional control does not establish safe overtravel of that switch.
Inspect and record the delivered dimensions before any rework. Then dry-fit
the parts and verify gentle electrical actuation and reliable release with
the actual switch. Adjust via an approved shim/cup revision if required.
