C5 enclosure R1 assembly views

The four PNGs show the final saved R1 printed geometry with the actual C5 carrier
PCB and its six assigned KiCad component model attachments. The 14 mm microphone
uses the original model attachment scale; hidden oversized import history from
the FreeCAD document is not used. Manufacturer and approximate model provenance
is unchanged. These renders do not replace dimensional inspection or the drawings.

assembled-isometric.png: closed enclosure, seven-hole microphone grille, screen
window, PTT plunger, two M2 screws and four M3 closure screws.

assembled-cutaway.png: assembled positions, with selected housing material and
the front half of the PTT mechanism removed. Amber identifies sectioned material.
The actual PCB and components are complete. The roof removal is an illustration,
not the shape to print. Some components are partly occluded by retained geometry;
the top-open view exposes their placement.

exploded-assembly.png: all four printed parts, installed electronics and reference
hardware separated vertically on their assembly axes. Relative to their mounted
positions, the presentation offsets are: base -55; inserts -40; PCB/components 0;
M2 nuts 60; stop cup 75; plunger 95; lid 130; M2/M3 screws 170 mm. These distances
are presentation separations, not operating clearances.

top-open-assembly.png: top view of the actual PCB inside the base, with the lid,
plunger, stop cup and closure screws removed for visibility.

C5-R1-assembly-views.png combines these four unchanged renders with titles.

Housing, plunger and cup colors distinguish parts in the illustrations. They
are not the ordered finish: the proposed first Craftcloud set is natural gray
unfilled MJF PA12 for all four printed parts.

The M2/M3 fasteners and heat-set inserts are representative standard dimensional
envelopes. Screw drives, head bevels and thread/knurl surfaces are illustrative;
no thread helices or knurl teeth are modeled. The displayed screw shafts are
diameter envelopes. Only the four printed parts are manufacturing CAD outputs.

Reproduction: build_final_views.py creates presentation-only WRL geometry and
KiCad boards. view-manifest.json records all source hashes, model attachments,
transforms and render settings. finalize_views.py records render hashes and makes
the contact sheet after visual inspection. Use KiCad 10 kicad-cli pcb render.
The source CAD, electronics project and manufacturing exports are not modified.

The boards in presentation/ include visual-only footprints. DO NOT fabricate them.
Use the source and Gerbers in ../pcb-fabrication for the actual carrier PCB.
