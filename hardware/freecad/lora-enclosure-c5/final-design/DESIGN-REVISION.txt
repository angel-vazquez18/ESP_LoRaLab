LoRa enclosure C5 R1 - engineering revision record
Date: 2026-11-24
Units: millimetres. Source PCB revision remains C5, electrically unchanged.

User requirements
1. Bring the lid closer to the ESP32/Heltec display without loading its glass.
2. Provide small direct microphone sound openings.
3. Improve printability and prepare a Craftcloud prototype order.
4. Supply actual-component assembly views, engineering drawings, BOM,
   PCB Gerbers/drills, and a CNC-shop handoff.

Native FreeCAD changes
- Headroom above PCB top reduced from 16 to 13; PCB top Z8, seam Z21,
  outer lid Z24.6. Main envelope 78.8 x 98.8 x 24.6.
- OLED reference glass top Z19.998719: 1.001281 below lid underside plane.
  Tallest modeled OLED screw leaves 0.698781 to actual lid geometry.
  The glass remains unloaded; no window lens, gasket or glass preload added.
- Existing central microphone hole changed from diameter 3 to 1.6.
  Six parametric diameter-1.6 holes added at radius 2.6, 60-degree increments,
  around (54.5,30.5). Seven holes total; minimum web 1.0; nominal open area
  14.074 mm2. This is a clear air path, not a validated acoustic seal.
- USB notch height now Headroom - USBBottomAbovePCB = 9.5, opening to seam
  at Z21. The lid forms its roof, eliminating the base's former 16 mm bridge.
- Lip clearance per side 0.3 -> 0.4; plunger guide radial clearance 0.3 -> 0.4
  (diameter-8 shaft in diameter-8.8 hole). No universal fit guarantee implied.
- Mounting insert selected: ruthex RX-M3x5x4, diameter 5 x length 4.
  Its pilot is diameter 4.4 x depth 5, matching manufacturer's installation
  diameter, length+1 depth and 1.3 minimum wall from the pilot in a diameter-7
  boss. Pilot floor Z1.4; narrower screw relief floor remains Z1.2.
- Closure screws changed from M3 x 25 to M3 x 22 ISO 4762 / DIN 912.
  Under-head plane Z24.6, nominal tip Z2.6, 3.8 nominal insert engagement,
  1.4 clearance above screw-relief floor. Standard heads are not recessed.
- PTT stop, flange and M2 mounting hardware move with the lowered lid.
  Nominal switch contact foot remains Z16.2 at release. Existing 0.2 free gap
  plus provisional 0.25 switch depression gives 0.45 total movement to stop.
  Stop shelf thickness remains 2; flange thickness remains 1.2.

Manufacturing decision
Craftcloud primary: unfilled MJF PA12, standard cleaned/depowdered finish,
one of each of four separate parts. No smoothing, coating or scaling without
critical-fit review. PETG/FDM is an alternative requiring support/orientation
review. The supplied neutral STL/3MF models contain no printer G-code.
CNC alternative: per-part STEP and reference DXF drawings for shop quotation
and process planning, with unfilled POM-C a candidate for shop review.
No machine-specific toolpaths, speeds/feeds or workholding are asserted.

Release scope
Digital geometry and fabrication exports are checked. This is a first-article
prototype order, not a physically qualified production assembly. Owned display
mounting height, microphone port location, cable boots, switch actuation/travel,
insert seating and delivered print tolerances must be checked on the prototype.
Default service tolerances do not by themselves control the 0.25 mm stop travel.
No load test, drop test, thermal qualification, RF test, acoustic performance,
environmental sealing or ingress protection rating is claimed.
