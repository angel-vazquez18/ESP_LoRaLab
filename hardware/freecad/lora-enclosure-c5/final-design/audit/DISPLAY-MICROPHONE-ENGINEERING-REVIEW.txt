DISPLAY AND MICROPHONE ENGINEERING REVIEW
2026-11-24 | Units: mm | Predictive CAD review, not a physical measurement

Display-to-lid stack

The carrier component mounting datum is PCB top Z8. The Heltec manufacturer
model is installed with its own PCB underside Z13, a 5 mm assembly gap which
the project explicitly marks as an unmeasured assembly assumption. The OLED
bracket and glass are a supplemental model, absent from the manufacturer STEP.
The supplement uses manufacturer drawing envelope dimensions, with approximate
glass thickness, corner profile, supporting posts and screw details.

Exact extracted supplement bounds:
  Frame: X13.7..32.3, Y41.55..74.55, Z17.538719..20.058719.
  Glass/window: X17.3..28.7, Y47.05..69.05, Z19.678719..19.998719.
  Highest decorative screw detail: Z20.301219.
  Enclosure window: 16.4 x 27 centred X23, Y58.05.
  Reference display window: 11.4 x 22, centred at the same XY.

The baseline 16 mm headroom puts the lid underside at Z24 and its outside face
at Z27.6. Its frame-to-lid vertical gap is 3.941281 and its glass is recessed
7.601281 below the outer lid face. This is spacious, rather than a close bezel.

The reviewed candidate changes headroom to 13 mm. The seam and underside become
Z21 and the outside face becomes Z24.6. The outer case height decreases by 3 mm.
The frame-to-lid vertical gap becomes 0.941281. The glass-to-underside reference
plane gap is 1.001281, and the glass remains 4.601281 below the outer lid face.
The minimum exact distance from the entire OLED supplement to lid material is
0.698781 at the tallest decorative screw detail. The aperture still clears the
11.4 x 22 window by 2.5 on each side. The aperture contains no lid above the
active screen, so the 1.001281 dimension is a vertical stack reference, not a
claim of direct glass contact or the minimum distance to the aperture rim.

The engineering intent is a close, unloaded bezel. A rigid printed face should
not be designed to press against this approximate glass model. No gasket or
screen compression is specified by the predictive candidate. The 5 mm header
assembly height and owned display/module revision should be checked before a
final production release. A prototype fit check is required to establish actual
clearance; the model is not a tolerance stack of measured parts.

Consequences of reducing enclosure height

Use M3 x 22 closure screws in place of the former M3 x 25. With a nominal screw
head seated at Z24.6, the 22 mm screw ends at Z2.6. That retains the original
3.8 mm insert engagement into an insert starting at Z6.4 and the original 1.4 mm
clearance above the blind relief floor Z1.2. Actual insert geometry and screw
length tolerances remain to be verified. The former 25 mm screw would be too
long and must not be carried over into this revision.

The predictive candidate uses USB opening height 9.5 above Z11.5, ending at the
new seam Z21. This eliminates the narrow roof bridge in the base. The opening
remains 16 mm wide. Actual plug overmould and insertion sweep remain unmodeled.

The PTT cup, flange and retaining guide move down 3 mm; the plunger contact foot
still starts Z16.2 and retains its 0.2 release gap to the switch. Cup top is Z21,
stop shelf top Z19.15, and cup underside Z17.15. M2 x 10 screw shafts run from
Z14.6 to 24.6; nuts run from Z15.55 to 17.15. The revised cup clears the modeled
switch by 1.411622. All checked M2 hardware envelopes clear the electronics.
At 0.45 downward plunger motion, cup distance is zero and solid overlap is zero.
At 0.46 downward motion, overlap is 0.918602 cubic mm. This confirms the geometric
stop. The stop's intended switch depression remains the provisional 0.25 mm
comparator value from the prior design; it is not a verified owned-switch rating.

Microphone acoustic access

The corrected project model is 14 mm in diameter, centred X54.5, Y30.5. In mounted
coordinates the modeled module acoustic face is Z12 and its highest feature is
Z12.3. Its overall model bounds include pins: X47.5..61.5, Y23.606346..37.393654,
Z5..12.3. The project specifies acoustic/solder face UP. The IC itself is a
bottom-port microphone: that fact alone does not determine which breakout face
must point to the lid. The project model shows the metal can below the module
and the assumed acoustic opening above it.

The candidate grille is seven through holes of diameter 1.6: one at the microphone
centre and six on a radius 2.6 regular hexagonal pattern. Minimum plastic ligament
between neighbouring holes is 1.0. Combined nominal open area is 14.074337 square
mm, approximately twice the baseline single diameter 3 hole. The central hole
retains a straight unobstructed line toward the assumed central microphone port;
the peripheral holes provide additional access to the common air cavity. The
holes are parallel to the normal of the lid and require no horizontal roof
bridges when the lid is printed outside-face down.

The nominal microphone face is 9 mm below the underside of the revised lid.
No acoustic tube, foam seal, tuning, sensitivity, environmental sealing or dust
ingress performance has been validated. The exact owned breakout port position,
diameter and installed height are explicitly unmeasured in the project. The
model's central diameter 0.9 port is a visual assumption, not a vendor dimension.
Keep any later mesh, foam or coating clear of the port and validate audio with
the assembled prototype before calling acoustic performance final.

CAD checks and scope

The candidate was recomputed in a separate headless FreeCAD document without
saving or changing the source FCStd. Checks used all 2,949 shapes in the corrected
detailed assembly STEP, translated +6.4814 in Z to the PCB top datum 8. Original
oversized microphone source objects retained in the FCStd were not used: that
document separately contains a visible MicrophoneActualScale replacement and
hidden original-source groups. New views must not render both copies.

All four printable candidate parts are valid, closed single solids. There are
no detected electronics penetrations for base, lid, plunger, cup, or the four M2
fastener envelopes. No checked printed-part pair has positive overlap volume.
The lid's electronics minimum clearances are 0.698781 OLED, 1.659247 Heltec,
1.8 amplifier, and 8.7 microphone. Intended PCB mounting regions are separate:
0.01 top silk and0.0932 substrate to lid support geometry. Base minimum to
non-PCB electronics remains 2.1 to switch and Heltec tails.

The source includes VRML-derived open presentation surfaces. The audit reports
surface penetration using intersection area and strict interior samples, as well
as positive volume for solid source shapes. These checks cannot certify actual
hardware tolerances, wiring, print shrinkage, strength or physical operation.

Evidence

geometry and source hashes. Its alternate mic pattern was exploratory only;
