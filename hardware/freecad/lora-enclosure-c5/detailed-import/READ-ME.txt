Detailed C5 exploded enclosure visualization

exploded-actual-kicad.png is rendered directly in KiCad from the current C5
board and its assigned models, using the original colors and geometry.
It includes the actual carrier outline, holes, pads, soldermask and silkscreen,
Heltec manufacturer STEP, project OLED/bracket supplement, MAX98357A breakout,
INMP441 module, fitted SW1 tactile switch and R8 axial resistor.

The enclosure surfaces come from the verified base/lid/plunger STL exports.
Only their presentation positions were changed. Their triangles were copied
into VRML without remodelling. The electronics retain their assigned positions
on the PCB. Exploded gaps are illustrative and do not represent assembly gaps.

This is the project's detailed CAD, not a scan of the user's hardware.
The OLED, audio modules and resistor include authored reference geometry;
their uncertainty is described in the source project's models/3D_MODEL_NOTES.txt.

FreeCAD interchange
C5-detailed-assembly.step contains the KiCad-exported board and all six assigned
model attachments. The four VRML-only attachments were translated to colored
AP214 presentation surfaces using their source vertices, polygons and materials.
The physical model scales were retained. KiCad's STEP substitution did not apply
the microphone's 1/2.54 footprint scale, so that factor was baked into its STEP
geometry. The corrected full assembly independently measures 14 mm diameter.
The conversion audit is source-project/vrml-step-conversion-audit.json.
These converted meshes are open visualization surfaces, not manufacturing solids.
The microphone's source triangulation includes zero-area and tiny orientation
artifacts; see the audit. Use the original KiCad-rendered PNG for faithful colors.
The FreeCAD bridge's STEP import flattens object names and display materials.

The exported board's finished Z envelope is -0.0832..1.5086 mm, and the actual
component mounting datum is Z1.5186. The FreeCAD assembly uses +6.4814 mm to
place that component datum at nominal PCB top Z8 (plus 55 mm for explosion).
The nominal enclosure spreadsheet still assumes a 1.6 mm carrier. No new
physical fit, RF, acoustic, or switch-travel validation is implied by this view.

LoRa-Enclosure-C5-Detailed-Exploded.FCStd is the separate FreeCAD presentation
document, one folder above this file. Its enclosure remains parametric; the
imported electronics are reference geometry grouped under KiCadAssembly.
Lid/plunger explosion offsets are 145/175 mm. The earlier simplified envelopes
and the uncorrected microphone interchange surfaces are hidden. The visible
MicrophoneActualScale shape was checked at 14 mm diameter and is valid in FreeCAD.
Use the original assembly model and verified STL files for manufacturing;
the detailed exploded document is arranged for visual inspection.

presentation-only copy. Do not use that copy as a fabrication board.
