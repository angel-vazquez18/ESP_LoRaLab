The project microphone WRL uses millimetre coordinates and a KiCad footprint
scale of 1/2.54. KiCad's STEP substitution path ignored that footprint scale.
The first substituted STEP therefore displayed a 35.56 mm microphone diameter.

The staged INMP441_ROUND_DETAILED_VISUAL.step now has the footprint factor baked
into its coordinates (1 mm per original WRL unit). Source WRL, original board,
and active project model assignments remain unchanged. Other converted WRLs
retain 2.54 mm per original WRL unit because their footprint scales are 1.

The complete C5-detailed-assembly.step was regenerated with:
  --subst-models --user-origin 100x190mm --include-pads --include-silkscreen
  --include-soldermask --force

Headless FreeCAD Part.insert independently confirmed a 14.000000000000014 mm
microphone X diameter. Corrected-assembly-check.json records bounds and hash.

The original live import's microphone consists of 647 objects named
C5_detailed_assembly1068 through C5_detailed_assembly1714 inclusive. Its raw
origin is (54.5,30.5,1.5186). To repair that earlier live view without reimporting,
scale only those objects by 1/2.54 about that origin, then retain the parent
assembly's normal/exploded placement. The regenerated STEP independently has
the same 647-object index range, but already contains corrected coordinates.

The presentation-only microphone surfaces retain the source-model CAD
orientation limitations noted in source-project/vrml-step-conversion-audit.json.
