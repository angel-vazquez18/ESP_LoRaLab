R6 enclosure engineering review
2026-11-25 | Review only; recommendations are not implemented revisions

ASSESSMENT
R6 is a credible first-article fit prototype. It has a useful mechanical
architecture and unusually good digital fit/export evidence. Production
readiness remains limited by unmeasured hardware, tolerance stacks, cable
retention/service motion, and the absence of physical load/acoustic/thermal
qualification. Shape validity is not a strength rating.

STRENGTHS TO PRESERVE
1. Load separation. The battery has a continuous 2.4 mm partition from the
   electronics and its closure screws are outside the pouch footprint.
   The PTT flange transfers stop load through the cup/fasteners to the lid.
   Speaker-retainer bosses provide a hard stop independent of speaker padding.
2. Actual assigned component geometry was used in interference checks. The
   new speaker envelope has 4.95 mm minimum component clearance; the retainer
   has 2.019 mm and its screw-shaft references 4.180 mm. Some purchased-part
   details, including OLED supplements and new speaker, remain approximations.
3. Repairable construction. Six printed parts and removable battery/speaker
   retainers permit servicing and replacement without adhesive disassembly.
4. Editable source and traceability. Native features, parameter tables, STEP,
   individual STL/3MF parts, source hashes and validation reports are retained.
5. Sensible process basis. Main walls and floor are 2.4 mm, main lid 3.6 mm,
   with rounded edges and an established unfilled MJF PA12 prototype process.
   These dimensions are design facts, not a proven impact capability.

PRIORITY 1: RESOLVE BEFORE THE RELEVANT HARDWARE TEST OR PRODUCTION RELEASE

A. Battery-only amplifier control (system issue exposed by integration)
   The accepted battery scope powers Heltec only; amplifier VIN remains on
   USB VBUS. The current local-node-homar sources still match the R4 review:
   the standard Heltec build enables GPIO48/SD_MODE without a VUSB check.
   With amplifier VDD absent, that can exceed the SD_MODE VDD+0.3 V rating.
   The actually flashed firmware has not been inspected.
   Action: verify a Heltec-profile speaker-disabled build for battery-only
   commissioning, or implement/test supply-aware shutdown including USB
   removal. Battery-powered audio needs a separately engineered power path.
   Do not connect VBAT directly to the current USB-fed amplifier rail.
   Evidence: R4 research/BATTERY-CONNECTION-REVIEW.txt; ADI source [1].

B. OLED and PTT tolerance stacks
   OLED displayed gap is 0.501281 mm from glass top to surrounding-rim datum;
   the aperture is open, with no protective lens above the glass. Modeled
   minimum frame distance is 0.441281 mm, screw clearance 0.698781 mm.
   Display/header mounting height has not been measured on the owned board.
   PTT switch depression is 0.25 mm, selected from comparable switches, not
   the actual switch's operating travel/allowable overtravel. Comparator
   travel variation of +/-0.1 mm and actuator height +/-0.2 mm are significant.
   Action: measure the mounted stack and actual switch actuation/release;
   establish minimum/maximum acceptable clearance and measured cup/shim height.
   Preserve a noncontact display fit; do not reduce the OLED gap further from
   nominal geometry alone. Use supplier-agreed critical tolerances/coupons.
   A supplier example quotes MJF accuracy with a +/-0.3 mm lower limit [2];
   this illustrates why 0.25/0.50 mm fits require explicit control. It is not
   a tolerance specification for every feature or the future Craftcloud shop.

C. SMA rotation and coax retention
   Mount is a provisional round DIA6.6 bore through a 2.4 mm seating wall.
   Nut/washer clamping carries the antenna, but no positive anti-rotation key
   is provided. Owned stud/shoulder/usable thread dimensions remain unknown.
   The current routing reference uses assumed DIA1.32 cable and R4.8 bends,
   while installation notes reserve provisional R10 for the unidentified kit.
   The tie slot conflicts with a simple planar tie on the illustrated route;
   no installed tie or complete surplus cable/service loop is qualified.
   Nominal cable clearance is 0.290 mm to a component and 0.440 mm to the lid.
   Action: measure the actual kit and bend requirement; add a replaceable
   rear-hex/D-flat capture if supported by the connector geometry. Reorient
   the anchor or add a smooth clip, model the installed retention and full
   slack route, and retain counter-hold access. Check repeated antenna fitting
   for rotation/loosening. Do not use the U.FL socket as mechanical restraint.

D. Battery and USB fit/charging limits
   The cell reference is a conservative 68 x 36 x 10 mm envelope. A 70 x 38 mm
   locating space, 1 mm pad allowance below and 3 mm above it are useful design
   margins, not a manufacturer-specified growth allowance. The actual battery
   plug overlaps a generic USB-overmould reservation in the existing study.
   Action: fit the owned pack, pad, straps and USB cable together; check full
   insertion and removal without squeezing the pouch or trapping leads.
   Confirm pack polarity and permitted charge current/temperature against the
   actual supplier. Heltec specifies 500 mA charging [3]; the reviewed pack
   listing does not establish its maximum allowed current. The supplied USB
   charging lead's 300 mA rating must not be treated as the cell's limit [4].

PRIORITY 2: IMPROVE DURABILITY, ASSEMBLY AND PERFORMANCE

E. Local thin sections and fastener installation
   Speaker nut pockets leave 0.8 mm of polymer above the nut; OLED screw reliefs
   leave 1.10 mm local roofs. These are likely locations to evaluate for preload,
   handling and impact sensitivity, not evidence that either has already failed.
   Supplier [2] lists 1 mm minimum walls; other providers have different rules.
   Action: obtain specific process approval for the 0.8 mm bearing roof or
   increase its local thickness with smooth transitions. Preserve the speaker
   hard-stop plane, update fastener engagement, and rerun clearances after any
   change. Evaluate local reinforcement around SMA/PTT loads before uniformly
   thickening the whole case. HP recommends ribs/fillets where appropriate [5].
   Use a printed insert/nut/slider coupon. Current main inserts use DIA4.4 x5
   pilots in DIA7 bosses (1.3 mm radial wall from pilot), with M3x22 screws.
   Battery-cover M3x6 screws have 0.9 mm nominal blind-end clearance. Clearly
   label hardware lengths; longer substituted screws may bottom out.

F. Speaker retention and lid service motion
   The 15 x10 x3.5 mm speaker envelope is based on approximate listing data.
   Its actual stationary rim, rear vents, leads and pad compression are unknown.
   A hard stop cannot compensate for an oversized speaker/padding stack.
   Speaker wire references clear components by only 0.350 mm and lid material
   by 0.400 mm. Drawn sections are about 53.47/44.55 mm of nominal 80 mm leads;
   those figures do not prove the lid can open fully while connected.
   Action: size pads from the real frame, add gentle strain relief and a
   deliberate service loop, and provide lid support during disconnection.
   Preserve direct SPK+/SPK- terminal wiring as requested. Test the entire
   closing/opening motion and screwdriver access, not just the closed pose.

G. Acoustic and speaker-power performance
   The speaker grille has nine DIA1.8 holes: calculated open area 22.90 mm2,
   about22% of the 13 x8 mm retainer/gasket window. This is not an acoustic
   efficiency rating or proof that more opening is needed. Ligaments are1.2 mm.
   The retained microphone face is about9 mm below the lid, without a sealed
   duct, and the microphone/speaker centers are16.5 mm apart in XY.
   Action: test a compliant microphone duct/gasket on the actual port and
   compare speech capture, rubbing noise, buzzing and intelligibility with
   the enclosure closed. Confirm intended half-duplex playback/capture control.
   Optimize the grille based on results while preserving structural webs.
   Validate an output-level limit for the1 W/8 ohm speaker: the amplifier can
   exceed1 W under its5 V datasheet conditions [1]. No measured acoustic fault
   or speaker damage is claimed by this review.

H. Environmental and thermal protection
   Open OLED, acoustic holes, USB access, seams and moving PTT do not establish
   an ingress rating. No installed thermal test is documented.
   Action: define indoor/outdoor requirements first. If needed, design a
   separately supported display lens, acoustic membranes and coordinated seals;
   recompute the complete fit and retest acoustics. Measure cell/charger/board
   temperatures during intended charging, transmit and audio duty cycles.
   Do not infer permissible battery temperature from PA12 heat resistance.

LOWER-PRIORITY MANUFACTURING AND CAD IMPROVEMENTS
- Keep MJF PA12 as the current prototype baseline, standard cleaned finish.
  Approve finishing changes against critical fits; polishing can remove
  material and degrade small features [2]. This is not a recommendation to
  order production quantities before first-article validation.
- Opposing cavities, small holes, thin ledges and internal details deserve
  separate support/access planning for FDM or a dedicated CNC-oriented revision.
  Do not treat STEP geometry as a qualified CNC toolpath.
- Consolidate hardware lengths, materials, critical dimensions and acceptance
  criteria into one R6 inspection sheet rather than relying on historical notes.
- Group superseded unlinked construction objects separately. The saved native
  file includes old speaker/antenna cutters and two superseded static fusions;
  these do not affect current final solids but complicate maintenance. Keep
  provenance while making current feature dependencies and exports unambiguous.

RECOMMENDED NEXT SEQUENCE
1. Measure owned OLED/header stack, switch travel, wrapped battery, plugs,
   SMA and speaker frame. Resolve battery-only enable behavior before powered
   commissioning. Establish provider tolerances and prototype acceptance loads.
2. Develop a targeted next revision: SMA anti-rotation/usable cable anchor,
   local retainer reinforcement if required, measured fits and service routing.
   Print critical fit coupons and one complete first article, not a batch.
3. Inspect dimensions and assembly; cycle PTT and lid service; test connector
   retention and fastener installation. Use inert mass for initial mechanical
   handling/drop trials. Then evaluate functional audio/RF and intended thermal
   duty cycles under an appropriate test plan. Release only measured results.
