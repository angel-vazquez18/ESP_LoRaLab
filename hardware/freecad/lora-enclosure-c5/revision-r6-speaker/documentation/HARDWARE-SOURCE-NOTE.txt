R6 speaker hardware basis - 2026-11-25

Selected fastener family: ordinary M2 x 0.4 hex nut, DIN 934 / ISO 4032.
Manufacturer source: Bossard assembly-tool table, M2 wrench size 4 mm.
https://media.bossard.com/ca-en/-/media/bossard-group/website/documents/technical-resources/en/f-077-en.pdf

Bossard BN 629 manufacturer dimensional sheet, article 1348418:
M2, width across flats 4.0 mm, maximum nut height 1.6 mm.
The manufacturer's sheet is hosted by its distributor:
https://media.distrelec.com/Web/Downloads/_t/ds/1241613_eng_tds.pdf

The R6 speaker plan was corrected from an unverified 3.5 mm AF assumption
to a 4.0 mm AF reference nut and 4.3 mm AF printed nut pocket. This is
0.3 mm nominal total clearance across flats, subject to printing tolerance.
The screw bosses and retainer outside boundary were widened to preserve
material around the larger recesses. The existing PTT stop cup uses a
separate already documented 4 mm AF nut arrangement and is not changed.

The M2 x 12 mm screw/0.3 mm washer stack is a reference selection for the
24.6 mm local lid top and 14.0-15.6 mm nut location. Verify the actual head,
washer and nut dimensions, full thread engagement and clearance on the
printed assembly. No tightening torque is qualified for the printed polymer.
