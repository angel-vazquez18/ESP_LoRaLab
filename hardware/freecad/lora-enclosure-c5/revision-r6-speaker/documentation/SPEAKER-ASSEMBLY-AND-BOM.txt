R6 internal speaker - assembly, wiring and incremental BOM
2026-11-25 | Nominal millimetres | Fit prototype

The enclosure supports one Alrhso speaker from ASIN B0GX6GZFJ2. The seller
gives approximately 15 x 10 x 3.5 mm, 1 W / 8 ohms and 80 mm leads. Its
connector is removed for direct wiring to the amplifier's green terminal.
The speaker is retained by the lid and a separate printed back retainer;
it is not mounted on the carrier PCB. No PCB or firmware change is made.

The gasket/pad CAD rings are fit references, not laser-cut patterns for an
unmeasured speaker. Trim to the actual stationary casing; do not cover the
diaphragm, rear vents, solder terminals or lead attachment. Choose thickness
by measured fit. Do not tighten the retainer to compensate for excessive pads.

MECHANICAL BASIS
Speaker envelope: X47.0-62.0 / Y42.0-52.0 / Z16.9-20.4; front faces +Z.
Cradle cavity: 15.6 x 10.6; nominal lateral clearance 0.3 on each side.
Front frame gasket: Z20.4-21.0. Back pad: Z16.4-16.9.
Retainer hard stop: Z16.4. Its window is 13 x 8, around a stationary rim.
Screw axes: X43.5,Y47 and X65.5,Y42; through bores diameter 2.4.
Nut recess: 4.3 AF and 1.8 actual depth, leaving a 0.8 roof above the nut.
Lid top at both screws: Z24.6; 0.3 washers raise the screw bearing plane
to Z24.9. Nominal 12 mm screw ends are at Z12.9; nuts occupy Z14.0-15.6.
Lead relief in the cradle right wall: Y45.5-48.5 / Z17.1-20.3.
Grille: nine holes, diameter 1.8; X51.5/54.5/57.5 and Y44/47/50.
The 3.0 mm pitch in both directions leaves 1.2 mm nominal webs between holes.
All dimensions are nominal. The native CAD controls full contours and fillets.

ASSEMBLY
1. Check the owned speaker body and actual rigid rim against the cradle.
   Remove print residue; check the grille is open and wire-relief edges smooth.
2. Fit the front gasket only to the rigid frame. Seat the speaker with its
   sound-emitting face toward the grille and its leads at the right relief.
3. Insert M2 nuts into the retainer from its underside. Fit rear padding only
   on the speaker's structural casing, keeping vents and terminals free.
4. Place the retainer against the printed boss stops. Insert washers and
   M2 x 12 screws from the outside of the lid, and engage both nuts. Tighten
   only enough to seat the assembly without crushing the speaker or polymer.
   No universal tightening torque is qualified for this printed assembly.
5. With all power disconnected, remove the unused speaker plug. Route the
   insulated pair through the relief, retaining service slack. Terminate the
   positive conductor at marked SPK+ and negative at marked SPK-. Check the
   actual terminal markings, accepted conductor size and stripping length.
   The model does not establish terminal left/right polarity or cavity details.
6. Neither speaker wire goes to GND, battery negative or chassis. Keep loose
   strands inside their own clamp and clear of adjacent contacts. Secure the
   insulated pair without loading the speaker solder joints or diaphragm.
7. Close slowly and confirm no wire pinch, retainer movement or diaphragm
   pressure. The lid is now tethered by the speaker wires: lift and support it
   only as far as the leads permit, then disconnect the terminal pair before
   full lid removal. Do not let the lid hang from the amplifier terminal.
8. Start playback at low volume. The 5 V MAX98357A can exceed the speaker's
   1 W rating; validate an appropriate audio-level limit under the installed
   gain/supply/firmware configuration before sustained loud playback.

VERIFICATION LIMITS
The enclosure, speaker envelope, fastener references and proposed leads are
checked against the actual assigned C5 component geometry. Listing dimensions
are approximate, and the exact rim, lead diameter, usable lead length and
terminal acceptance are not manufacturer-qualified by this model. Measure
the hardware and try a printed prototype before treating the fit as released.
The proposed drawn lead portions are about 53.47 and 44.55 mm. Surplus lead
placement is not modeled; the nominal 80 mm supplied leads do not establish
that the lid can open fully while connected. Confirm service access on hardware.
Acoustic output, buzzing, microphone coupling and the audio power limit still
require physical commissioning. The amplifier remains USB powered under the
existing project arrangement; this mount does not add battery power to it.
