R6 interactive enclosure inspection

The viewer retains the project-assigned PCB/component geometry and displays
the new enclosure-mounted speaker, back retainer, acoustic grille and direct
lead route to the MAX98357A speaker output. The speaker does not mount to
the PCB. Speaker hardware and leads are reference envelopes, not an exact
manufacturer-supplied 3D model. Do not infer wire polarity from the viewing
angle: connect the speaker only across SPK+ and SPK-, never SPK- to GND.

Assembled uses the complete shapes. Cutaway makes capped Boolean cuts of
the housing for inspection only. Speaker mount inspection uses complete
transparent housing so the cradle and grille can be seen from either side.
These viewing cuts are not holes in the manufacturing files.

Exploded offsets are presentation separations, not dimensions. The speaker,
gasket, back retainer and reference hardware separate from the lid. Leads
translate rigidly with the speaker and are disconnected from the amplifier;
they are not stretched to bridge the presentation gap. The coax similarly
translates with the base and is disconnected from the PCB.

The nine retained clearance views are regenerated from the R6 source shapes
and actual component assembly; source hashes and endpoint witness checks
are in focus/FOCUS-MESH-VALIDATION.json and dimensions/DIMENSION-VALIDATION.json.
The OLED 0.50 mm dimension is an inner-rim height datum offset, not the minimum
solid-edge distance. Battery lower space is a padding allowance.

The R5 viewer and all source manufacturing files remain unchanged by this
derived presentation. The current geometry source status and source hashes
are recorded in SOURCE-INPUTS.json and the validation reports.
