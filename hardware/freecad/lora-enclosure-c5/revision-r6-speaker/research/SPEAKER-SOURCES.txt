1. Exact user-selected product listing (seller's primary product description)
https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GX6GZFJ2
Alrhso four-pack, ASIN B0GX6GZFJ2. The listing specifies an approximate
15 x 10 x 3.5 mm body, approximately 80 mm cable, 1 W rating and 8 ohm
impedance. The user's dimensioned image independently shows 15 x 10 mm
and 80 mm leads. The seller describes the connector as JST-PH1.25mm-2 pin;
this nomenclature is not treated as a verified connector specification.
The user will remove that connector and terminate the leads at the amp.
The listing does not supply mechanical tolerances, frame/rim section,
diaphragm excursion, rear-protrusion dimensions or wire strain-relief size.
The reference CAD therefore cannot be described as a manufacturer 3D model.
Seller statements about logic supply compatibility or not needing an
amplifier are not adopted as electrical specifications for this passive load.

2. Amplifier manufacturer's primary datasheet
https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/max98357a-max98357b.pdf
Analog Devices MAX98357A/MAX98357B, Electrical Characteristics page 5,
Pin Description page 15, and typical application circuits.
The speaker load connects between OUTP and OUTN. OUTP is the positive
speaker output; OUTN is the negative speaker output, separate from GND.
At VDD=5 V, gain=12 dB and an 8 ohm + 68 uH test load, typical output
power is 1.4 W at 1% THD+N and 1.8 W at 10% THD+N. The chip's overload
protection is not a 1 W loudspeaker power limiter.

3. Breakout manufacturer's implementation guidance
https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-max98357-i2s-class-d-mono-amp/pinouts
Adafruit's MAX98357A guide describes the speaker output as bridge-tied:
neither speaker conductor is grounded. Its screw-terminal speaker wiring
and 5 V / 8 ohm output figures agree with the IC datasheet. This is a
primary implementation example, not identification of the user's exact
green-terminal module or its terminal pitch, clamp capacity or torque.

Design implications and project choices (engineering interpretation)
- One 8 ohm speaker, mounted mechanically to the enclosure rather than PCB.
- With system power off, remove the unused speaker plug and terminate the
  speaker's positive lead at module SPK+ and negative at SPK-. Confirm the
  module markings; color/terminal orientation alone is not authoritative.
  Neither speaker lead connects to PCB GND, battery negative or chassis.
- Use the module terminal's actual accepted wire size and stripping length;
  the clamp must retain conductor, with no loose strands bridging terminals.
  Provide strain relief to insulated wire, not its solder joint or diaphragm.
- The USB-powered amplifier can deliver more than this speaker's nominal
  1 W rating. Begin at low volume and establish an appropriate audio-level
  limit under the actual gain, supply and firmware configuration before
  sustained playback. Enclosure integration does not validate that limit.
  For reference only, 1 W in an ideal 8 ohm resistance corresponds to
  sqrt(1*8)=2.83 V RMS of audio across the load. This is not a raw class-D
  switching-waveform measurement prescription or a guaranteed safe level
  for an uncharacterized speaker over its entire frequency range.
- Support the stationary perimeter/body with compliant isolation; leave
  the sound-emitting face unobstructed and avoid diaphragm compression.
  Acoustic leakage, buzzing and output level require an assembled prototype.
- 80 mm is a nominal supplied lead length, not 80 mm of usable installed
  route: reserve termination length and opening/service slack. Do not make
  a drawing imply a dimensioned supplier guarantee for unmeasured leads.
- Preserve the existing carrier PCB and amplifier supply arrangement.
  This revision changes enclosure accommodation, not the electronics.
