LoRa C5 enclosure - R4 separate LiPo battery compartment
2026-11-24 | Millimetres | Prototype mechanical integration

Accepted scope
Store the user-selected Meshnology 3.7 V 3000 mAh 103665 protected LiPo in
the enclosure and provide a cable route to Heltec V4 JP2/BAT. The user chose
compartment and Heltec connection only. The carrier PCB, firmware and the
USB-fed amplifier supply are not changed by this revision.

Battery reference
The user's product image labels 68 x 35 x 10 mm and a 66 mm lead. The design
uses a conservative 68 x 36 x 10 mm packaging envelope because the 103665
designation suggests a 36 mm cell width. This is a packaging reference,
not manufacturer 3D CAD or a measured maximum including expansion/tolerances.
Verify the actual pack, terminations and maximum thickness on a prototype.

Mechanical changes
A 14 mm-deep rear bay is added below the retained original floor. A separate
2.4 mm back cover closes it. Shell size is 78.8 wide x 104.0 maximum long x
41.0 deep, including the local rear cable channel but excluding button and
screw-head projections. The main body length remains 98.8; the local cable
channel extends it by 5.2 mm. The original shell depth was 24.6 mm.

The cell envelope is X17..53, Y11..79, Z-13..-3. Guides provide a nominal
70 x 38 mm locating space. There is 1 mm below the nominal cell for a soft
insulating support and 3 mm above it; these are design allowances, not a
manufacturer-approved swelling specification. The original 2.4 mm floor
remains a continuous barrier beneath the carrier and above the pouch.

Use two soft, nonconductive 10 mm-wide straps through the rounded cover
slots. Retain without squeezing the pouch. Keep the strap overlap in the
available clearance and check its actual thickness. Deburr/finish all
battery-facing edges and route the lead without trapping it at the cover.
The four rear screws and insert posts are outside the pouch footprint.

Rear closure hardware
4 x M3x6 button-head screws, with 3.6 mm nominal thread engagement after the
2.4 mm cover. Do not substitute M3x8 against these blind holes.
4 x ruthex RX-M3x5x4-compatible inserts (5 mm OD, 4 mm length), using 4.4 mm
pilot diameter and 4.5 mm blind depth. Verify the purchased insert/process.
Screw-tip clearance to the modeled blind-pilot end is 0.9 mm.
Existing top closure and PTT hardware remain unchanged.

The R3 lid retains the nominal 0.50 mm OLED inner-rim gap, 2.10 mm glass to
local exterior face, and screw reliefs. The PCB, Heltec and PTT datums remain
fixed. Main closure screws remain M3x22, with the existing four inserts.

Cable route and commissioning limits
JP2/BAT faces +Y near the USB end. The new rear channel avoids any PCB hole
and provides an approximately 43 mm reference route for the pictured 66 mm
lead. Actual mating-housing protrusion, keying, polarity, minimum bend radius
and useful service slack are not established by the photographs. Use a
correctly wired extension only if needed after checking these conditions.
The supplied USB charging cable is external and has no place in this bay.

The assumed battery plug clears printed material and modeled electronics,
but overlaps the existing generic USB-cable-overmould reservation. The actual
simultaneous battery-plug/USB-cable fit is NOT qualified. Check with the owned
plug and USB cable, using a suitable low-profile cable if necessary, before
ordering production quantities. Do not force connectors or pinch the wires.

The normal audio firmware drives GPIO48/AMP_SD HIGH at boot without detecting
USB power. With USB absent, the amplifier has no supply, and this SD input
condition is outside the amplifier specification. Battery-only commissioning
requires a verified speaker-disabled Heltec firmware build that holds AMP_SD
LOW. No firmware change or flashing was performed in this mechanical scope.
See research/BATTERY-CONNECTION-REVIEW.txt for exact source lines and evidence.

Verify actual connector polarity: Heltec JP2 pin 1 is VBAT positive and pin 2
is GND. Matching pitch alone does not establish keying or polarity. Heltec's
nominal charger is approximately 500-540 mA; the pack's maximum permissible
charge current was not published in the reviewed product information and
must be confirmed before qualifying charging through the Heltec.

Printing / revision control
This revision has FIVE printed parts: revised base, R3 lid, PTT plunger,
PTT stop cup and new battery cover. Use the R4 Craftcloud upload folder as
one set; the earlier four-part R1/R2/R3 bundles omit this battery compartment.
The prototype process remains unfilled MJF PA12. The base has opposing
cavities around a retained partition; ordinary FDM may need removable
supports and is not represented as support-free by this handoff.
No slicing, machining G-code, order submission or physical qualification
is included. The STEP contains printable parts only; battery, PCB and
harness reference geometry are excluded from manufacturing exports.
