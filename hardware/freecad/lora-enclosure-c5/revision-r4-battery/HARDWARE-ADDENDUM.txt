R4 hardware and assembly addendum
2026-11-24

Added for battery compartment:
1 Meshnology protected 3.7 V 3000 mAh 103665 LiPo, verified actual dimensions.
4 M3x6 button-head screws for removable rear cover.
4 RX-M3x5x4-compatible M3 inserts, OD5 x length4 mm.
2 soft nonconductive retaining straps, nominal 10 mm wide; cut to fit with
  secure overlap and no pouch compression (approximately 160 mm starting
  length per strap, confirm actual path/thickness).
1 smooth nonconductive support pad up to the allocated 1 mm thickness,
  approximately 36 x 68 mm, compatible with the cell and printing material.
Optional verified-polarity 1.25 mm two-pin service extension if the actual
  included 66 mm lead cannot provide adequate assembly/service slack.

Retained hardware:
4 M3x22 top closure screws and 4 existing RX-M3x5x4 inserts.
2 M2x10 PTT stop-cup screws and 2 M2 nuts.
All existing electronics and external speaker/coax harness items.
Total M3 inserts in a complete R4 assembly: 8 (four top, four rear).

Suggested assembly order:
1 Fit/check inserts before introducing the battery; remove all debris.
2 Trial-fit the actual battery/USB mating plugs and route with power off.
3 Place the insulating support, cell and soft straps on the rear cover.
4 Route the lead through the rear channel and connect only after verifying
  polarity, connector fit and the commissioning conditions in the review.
5 Close with M3x6 rear screws, checking that no lead or pouch is trapped.
6 Retain the existing PCB/top assembly procedure and verify OLED/PTT motion.

The connector, firmware and charge-current conditions in the accompanying
electrical review remain prerequisites to battery-powered commissioning.
