This CNC alternative has not been qualified with a particular stock or machine.
The shop must choose and quote an appropriate insulating engineering-plastic
stock, confirm material behavior at thin features and inserts, and obtain
agreement before substituting material or changing geometry. No equivalence
between a machined billet and an MJF build is implied.

Proposed operations for shop planning
1. Base: fixture with support for the thin retained floor. Machine PCB-side
   cavity/mount details, then re-register for the opposed battery cavity and
   rear insert pilots. Reorient as necessary for side openings and rear chase.
2. Cover: machine battery side and guide ribs; flip for 1 mm-deep strap channels.
   Machine the 2.4 x 12 rounded strap slots and dia 3.4 screw clearances.
3. Lid: top display recess and ports, then underside clamps/reliefs/tongue.
4. Plunger: lathe or mill the shaft, flange and foot with suitable small-part
   holding; do not alter the lower flange stop plane.
5. Cup: cavity, foot bore, mounting lugs and holes; retain the 2 mm stop shelf.
These are setup concepts, not numbered CAM operations or released toolpaths.

DFM decisions before CAM release
- Opposed cavities, narrow passages, sharp internal corner transitions, the
  rear hood and thin features need a tool-access and workholding review.
- Stock allowance, cutter radius, corner reliefs, fixturing tabs, work offsets,
  cut order, feeds/speeds, coolant compatibility and postprocessor are shop tasks.
- Do not silently add fillets/dogbones. Check any proposed changes against
  battery retention, electronics clearance and PTT load/stop surfaces.
- Preserve 2.4 mm PCB separator, 1.4 mm residual cover floor under channels,
  and 1.1 mm minimum local lid roofs. Support thin sections during machining.
- All dimensions are nominal; no blanket manufacturing tolerance is released.
  Propose and agree capability for the 0.4 mm locating/sliding gaps, dia 4.4
  insert pilots, PTT travel and nominal 0.50 mm OLED rim clearance.
- STEP is authoritative for full 3D surfaces. DXF curves are sampled polylines
  at 0.025 mm deflection, not exact analytical arcs and not 2.5D cut profiles.
  Hidden-reference and note layers must never be interpreted as cut paths.

Required shop deliverables before running a machine
Chosen material/stock, setup sheets, tool list, CAM simulation and collision/
stock-rest verification, controller-specific posted program and first-article
inspection plan. No G-code, offsets, spindle commands or CNC machine selection
are fabricated in this package.

Fit and electrical acceptance
Check the actual cell/lead envelope, connector keying/polarity, simultaneous
USB cable fit, strap/padding compression, screw/insert engagement and the OLED
stack on a first article. Do not replace rear M3 x 6 screws with M3 x 8.
The PTT 0.25 mm operating travel is representative, not the installed switch's
verified allowable travel. Qualify actuation and stop engagement physically.
CAD/file verification is not cell swelling, thermal, drop, ingress or acoustic
qualification. The separate C5 battery electrical review remains applicable:
the C5 carrier's amplifier remains USB-powered, and the reviewed local-node-homar
audio firmware needs a verified speaker-disabled Heltec configuration for
battery-only commissioning. Confirm pack charge-current limits before charging
through Heltec. No upstream bench hardware or firmware changes are made here.
