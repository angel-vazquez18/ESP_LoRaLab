LoRa C5 enclosure - R2 OLED recessed bezel
2026-11-24 | All dimensions in mm | Prototype revision

Purpose
Bring the local exterior window rim closer to the OLED glass without moving
the electronics or reducing the existing internal clearances.

Change from R1
A rectangular exterior pocket removes 2.0 mm of lid thickness around the
existing 16.4 x 27 open window, centered at X23, Y58.05. The pocket floor is
20.4 x 31 at Z22.6. Its 45-degree sides expand to 24.4 x 35 at Z24.6.
A 2.0 mm-wide ledge surrounds the aperture, with 1.6 mm remaining thickness.
The main lid remains 3.6 mm thick outside the recess. No protective lens,
gasket, display compression, or new mounting hardware is introduced.

Nominal vertical fit
OLED glass top:                         Z19.998719
Lid underside reference plane:          Z21.000000
Recessed window-rim face:               Z22.600000
Surrounding main lid outside face:      Z24.600000
Glass to recessed local exterior face:   2.601281 (previously 4.601281)
Glass to underside reference plane:      1.001281
Glass to surrounding main outside face:  4.601281 (unchanged)
Highest modeled OLED screw to lid:       0.698781 (unchanged; off section)

The 1.001281 mm dimension references the inner plane around an open aperture;
it is not a gap to a continuous ceiling directly above the active screen.
R2 only removes exterior material. Carrier, Heltec, base, PTT plunger, stop
cup, microphone vents, cable exits and screw datums retain their R1 positions.

Parametric controls
Parameters B73: local bezel thickness = 1.6
Parameters B74: ledge width = 2.0
Parameters B75: recess depth = B9 - B73 = 2.0
Parameters B76: recessed exterior plane = B44 + B73 = 22.6
Parameters B77: Boolean cutter overrun = 0.1
OLEDRecessFloor and OLEDRecessTop are expression-driven Part planes.
OLEDBezelRecess is a native solid ruled Part loft linked into LidCutters.
native-revision-operations.json records the actual direct MCP construction.
Keep local thickness positive and below the full lid thickness. Changes to
the parameter values require renewed geometry, interference and mesh checks.

Printing and revision control
This R2 lid replaces the R1 lid; the R1 base, plunger and stop cup remain
geometrically unchanged. Do not use the old R1 lid in an order for R2.
The previously prepared R1 Craftcloud ZIP has not been replaced by this
focused revision. Use the R2 lid plus the unchanged R1 other three parts.
The STEP and lid meshes use assembly coordinates, not slicer-bed origins.
Reorient/place the lid on the build plate in a slicer if printing locally.
The intended prototype process remains unfilled MJF PA12; the 45-degree
recess transitions avoid a sharp thin edge around the screen opening.

Limits
The OLED frame/glass and screw details are supplemental model geometry;
glass thickness and installed module spacing have not been measured on the
owned hardware. These dimensions are nominal CAD clearances, not a measured
tolerance stack. Confirm the display and header fit on a prototype before
production. The local rim is not a structural load or impact qualification.
No firmware, PCB or Gerber design is changed by this enclosure revision.
