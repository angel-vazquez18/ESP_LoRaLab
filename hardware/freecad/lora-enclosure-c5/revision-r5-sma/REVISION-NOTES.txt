R5 SMA antenna bulkhead mount - 2026-11-24
Parametric fit prototype; millimetres.

The latest user reference specifies an SMA female bulkhead, an SMA male
915 MHz antenna and an IPEX/U.FL pigtail. The temporary direct-spring-antenna
concept is not adopted. The earlier user image supplies 50 x 8 mm antenna
and 169 mm pigtail envelopes; connector mounting dimensions remain unmeasured.

The SMA axis is X47, Z14, pointing +Y through the USB end wall. A nominal
6.6 mm round bore and 2.4 mm seating wall support the connector's shoulder,
washer arrangement and nut. An integral rounded backing reinforces the
wall while an 11.2 mm rear relief leaves access to the fixed connector body.
This is a round-bore, nut-and-washer clamped mount, not a keyed hex capture.
Counter-hold the fixed body while tightening the mounting nut or antenna.

A separate rounded anchor accommodates a loose nominal 2.5 mm cable tie.
Keep the tie head beside the cable and preserve service slack; do not clamp
the fine coax hard or transfer antenna rotation to the board's U.FL socket.
The pigtail route, bend limit, nut torque and antenna thread engagement need
checking with the owned hardware. No strength, drop, sealing or RF qualification
is implied by nominal CAD clearance checks.

The old split coax exit is closed in both base and lid. Use the R5 base and
lid together. The R4 battery cover, PTT plunger and stop cup are retained with
identical geometry. The battery compartment and 0.50 mm OLED datum offset
are retained. PCB files and electrical design are not changed by this revision.

Key editable cells in the native Parameters table:
B40 SMA centre X; B41 bore diameter; B96 centre Z; B97 rear relief diameter;
B98 seating thickness; B99 backing depth; B100 backing width.
Native primitive dimensions and fillet radii also remain editable.
Reference antenna/connector objects are illustrative and excluded from all
print and STEP exports. The native document retains the development history.

Printing package: five parts, quantity one each, unfilled MJF PA12 as in R4.
Use ONE format per part (STL or 3MF), millimetres, scale 100 percent.
Craftcloud upload files are neutral models, not sliced printer G-code.
Verify the owned bulkhead fits the 6.6 mm hole, rear relief and 2.4 mm seat
before placing a manufacturing order. No order is placed by this task.
