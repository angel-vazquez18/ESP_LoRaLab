R5 INTERACTIVE COAX ROUTING REFERENCE

The rendered coax is a proposed routing envelope, not a measured model of the
owned antenna lead. A 1.32 mm diameter and R4.8 mm centerline bends are used;
the inside bend radius is 4.14 mm. Confirm actual cable diameter, flexibility,
minimum bend radius, usable lead length and connector strain before assembly.

The cable starts at the representative SMA rear face, (47,82,14) mm. The
illustrative plug lands on the actual assigned LoRa socket mating face at
(23.00004,39.14914,15.81875) mm. This is the upper LoRa socket, not the separate
underside 2.4 GHz connector. The plug-to-socket distance is approximately
0.000001 mm, within source-coordinate roundoff.

The proposed centerline measures 123.9255 mm. The earlier photograph's 169 mm
dimension refers to a pigtail assembly; it is not evidence of available cable
centerline length. Any surplus lead/slack is not modeled or qualified here.

Exact geometric checks found no positive-volume intersection with the 2,949
assigned PCB/component shapes or the five printable enclosure parts. Proposed
cable clearances are 0.6634 mm to the base, 0.4400 mm to the lid, and 0.2900 mm
to the nearest actual component (source index 2136, amplifier region).
The coax touches the representative SMA rear face intentionally. This is a
geometric visualization check, not a cable-fit, RF or impact qualification.

The cable passes near/above the retention anchor. The slot is oriented along X,
while the illustrated cable turns largely along X at that position. A simple
planar tie would intersect this route. No installed tie is depicted: select
the actual loose tie orientation and adjust service slack when fitting real
hardware. The SMA nut/washer clamp provides the rigid antenna mounting.

Exploded view translates the cable rigidly with the base by -30 mm in Z; the
PCB connection is intentionally shown disconnected. No cable stretching is
implied by the exploded presentation.

Reproduce using FreeCAD's bundled Python and build_route.py. Missing read-only
BREP caches are regenerated by probe_route.py and removed after validation.
Source CAD/STEP files are never saved or altered. route-mesh-data.json contains
the display mesh; coax.brep and coax_plug.brep retain the final derived shapes.
