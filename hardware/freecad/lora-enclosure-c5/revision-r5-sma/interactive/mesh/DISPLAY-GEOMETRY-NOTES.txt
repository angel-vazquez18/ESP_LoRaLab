R5 interactive display geometry

Source of printed parts: saved LoRa-Enclosure-C5-R5-SMA.FCStd.
Source of PCB and all 2,949 assigned component shapes: corrected KiCad STEP
C5-detailed-assembly.step, translated +6.4814 mm on Z as in R4.
Unchanged R4 display meshes are retained without retessellation or decimation.
Their source-file hashes, source index coverage, and the old mesh hash are checked.
Retained printed parts and the battery envelope also pass native Boolean
symmetric-difference checks between the released R4 and R5 CAD.

Display-only cutaway:
- Base and battery cover: remove Y<30 mm, plus X>70 mm where Y<84 mm.
- Lid: same cuts, plus X18..66, Y30..96, Z19..40 mm roof window.
- Newly created planar cut faces are colored as sections.
- PCB and assigned components remain complete in overview cutaway.
- All nine retained R4 local focus volumes have zero native R4/R5 symmetric
  difference for base, lid, cover, PTT plunger, PTT stop cup, and battery envelope.
These are inspection cuts, not physical changes or manufacturing exports.

SMA body, washer, nut, antenna coupler, and antenna are provisional envelopes
from R5 reference objects. Native washer/nut envelopes receive cylindrical
internal display voids; the coupler receives a partial internal display void.
These additions avoid a misleading filled washer/nut while making no claim
about the owned connector's precise threads or internal construction.

Coax and its IPEX plug are provisional reference geometry from the separately
audited routing directory. The path terminates at the actual top LoRa IPEX
socket, not the underside 2.4 GHz socket. Its length is an illustrative route,
not proof that the user's supplied lead has that usable length.

All display coordinates use 0.001 mm rounding. Small collapsed triangles from
the original tessellation may be omitted; no geometric decimation is applied.
The schema2 delta/varint transport round-trip checks every coordinate and index.
Use native CAD, STEP and manufacturing meshes for fabrication or metrology.
