R5 interactive enclosure inspection

The viewer uses the saved R5 native CAD with the corrected KiCad component
assembly. The R4 viewer remains unchanged. Native CAD and manufacturing
files are not modified by this presentation.

Assembled uses full geometry. Cutaway uses real Boolean cuts of the printed
shell to expose the PCB, battery bay, rear SMA backing and cable corridor.
These are viewing sections, not new holes in the production enclosure.
Exploded uses presentation translations: the coax moves rigidly with the
base and is disconnected from the PCB; washer, nut and antenna separate
along the SMA axis. Exploded separations are not clearance dimensions.

SMA mount and Coax route buttons smoothly frame their respective regions.
Manual rotation and zoom remain available; Back to overview restores the
previous view. The nine clearance buttons retain their local CAD sections.
The 0.50 mm OLED dimension is glass-top to the inner-rim height datum, not
the nearest solid-edge distance. Battery lower space is a padding allowance.

All assigned KiCad geometry is retained without geometric decimation.
Display positions are quantized to 0.001 mm, then losslessly compressed.
The battery, SMA hardware, antenna, mating plug and cable are fit references.
The coax is a provisional routing illustration, not a measured model of
the owned 169 mm pigtail assembly. Cable diameter, usable lead length,
remaining service slack and allowed bend radius must be checked on hardware.
The path terminates at the top LoRa IPEX port, not the underside 2.4 GHz port.
The tie anchor is shown; an installed tie is not. The loose tie arrangement
around the proposed cable route remains to be resolved with real hardware.

The build, mesh, routing, dimension, focus and browser validation reports
record source hashes and the checks performed. No ordering or publishing
is performed by this viewer update.
