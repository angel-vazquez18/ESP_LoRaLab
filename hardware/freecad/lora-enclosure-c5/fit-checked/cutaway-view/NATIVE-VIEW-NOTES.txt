Assembled enclosure cutaway with actual project models

LoRa-Enclosure-C5-Assembled-Cutaway.FCStd is a separate presentation document.
The lid remains at Z24..27.6 and the actual component mounting datum is Z8.
All exploded offsets have been removed. The original manufacturing files and
the earlier exploded view are unchanged.

The overview removes selected roof, front-wall and right-wall material. Amber
surfaces in the KiCad renders identify enclosure section faces. The cup,
plunger, screws and nuts are cut at the Y9 centre plane. This exposes the
stop mechanism; it does not change the underlying manufactured geometry.
The actual PCB and all project-assigned electronics models remain complete
in the overview. Fastener threads and drive details are representative.

ptt-assembled-section.png is a front section through the installed mechanism.
Orange: plunger. Green: stop cup. Light gray: lid. Gray: M2 screws/nuts and
the actual assigned SW1 model. Purple: actual carrier substrate, pads and masks.
Dark blue: a local section of the case floor. PCB supports are outside this
local section; the full overview shows the mounting structure.

The PCB detail comes from actual imported indices889..1067 and2947/2948,
translated +6.4814 mm and clipped to X38..64, Y9..22. The switch is the actual
project-assigned SW1 model at that same mounted datum. Its section is valid.
PCB mask layers include open visualization surfaces, as in the source model.

The nominal CAD pose has a 0.20 mm foot-to-switch gap and 0.45 mm flange-to-stop
gap. The nominal 0.25 mm switch depression setting remains the comparable-part
assumption documented in the parent FIT-REVIEW.txt; this view adds no new
physical travel or strength validation.

To show the saved local detail in FreeCAD, hide CutawayBase, CutawayLid and
KiCadAssembly; show PTTDetailPCB, PTTDetailSwitch, PTTDetailFloor and PTTDetailLid;
keep the six CutawayPTT/fastener objects visible, choose Front, then Fit All.
All detail objects are hidden in the saved overview.
