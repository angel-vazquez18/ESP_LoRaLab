Assembled enclosure cutaway — 2026-11-24

This is a sectioned presentation of the closed enclosure. There are no exploded
offsets: the PCB, actual assigned component models, lid and PTT mechanism remain
at their assembled positions. The lid underside remains at Z24 and its top at
Z27.6. The native manufacturing CAD and exports have not been changed.

Amber faces indicate the enclosure's cut surfaces. The orange half-section is
the plunger; green is the stop cup. The two gray half-sections show M2 screws
and nuts at their installed positions. The rear half of the local lid around
the PTT and screw holes is retained to expose how the parts nest underneath it.
The small nominal release/stop gaps are model geometry, not printer tolerances.

The PCB and all six original electronics model attachments are left complete,
with source geometry, positions and colors unchanged. The housing and four PTT
fasteners are presentation sections only. Fastener envelopes use the reviewed
M2 dimensions; cross-drive and bevel details are representative, not supplied
manufacturer CAD, and threads are not modeled.

The removed housing comprises the front section before Y9, the right section
beyond X70, and the lid roof over X6..64,Y-10..82, except the local PTT lid patch
at X38..64,Y9..18. The floor is retained below Z1.2. Cut details are recorded in
cutaway-manifest.json. All parts other than the PCB/components are rendered at
Z offset -8 relative to KiCad's board-top coordinate system, giving zero
physical explosion.

This cutaway is an illustration, not a manufacturing model or fabrication PCB.
Use LoRa-Enclosure-C5-Fit-Checked.FCStd and the four STL files in the parent
folder for the original closed geometry. Fit validation and physical limitations
