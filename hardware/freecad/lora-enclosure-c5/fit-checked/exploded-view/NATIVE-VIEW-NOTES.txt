Updated FreeCAD exploded view with PTT hardware

LoRa-Enclosure-C5-Exploded-With-Hardware.FCStd is a presentation document.
Use the parent fit-checked CAD and STL files for printing.

The overview retains the actual imported PCB and component reference geometry,
including the corrected 14 mm microphone. The original KiCad renderer preserves
their colors in exploded-updated-actual-kicad.png. Standard-dimension M2 hardware
has simplified threads and drive details; it is not vendor-supplied source CAD.

Overview Z offsets from mounted positions, in mm:
base 0; PCB 55; nuts 115; stop cup 130; plunger 150; lid 185; screws 225.
Each screw and nut remains on its mounting axis at X42/Y9 or X60/Y9.

ptt-exploded-detail.png uses a local 26 x 18 mm section of the actual lid,
including the PTT guide and both fastener holes. It is an illustration of the
lid region, not an extra printed plate. Its section source, window and result
are hidden in the saved overview. The detail uses compact offsets: nuts -14,
cup 0, plunger 20, lid section 40, screws 65. All explosion gaps are illustrative.

Assembly: insert the plunger through the lid from inside, slide the cup over
its foot and flange, and attach with two M2 x 10 pan-head screws from outside
and two M2 full nuts under the cup. See the parent FIT-REVIEW.txt for details.
