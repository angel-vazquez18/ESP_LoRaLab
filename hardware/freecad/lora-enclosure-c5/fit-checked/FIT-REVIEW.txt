LoRa enclosure C5 - actual-model fit review and PTT stop revision
All dimensions in mm. Revision date: 2026-11-24.

Baseline actual-component clearance check

The original assembled enclosure was checked against all 2,949 objects in the
corrected detailed KiCad assembly, including the 14 mm microphone. The assembly
was translated +6.4814 in Z to put its component datum at PCB top Z8. Semantic
STEP labels identify 12 component/material families; exploded offsets were not
used. Exact geometry distance and intersection checks found no component-to-case
penetrations. Baseline results are in baseline-actual-clearance.json.

Smallest hardware clearances to the base: SW1 tails 2.100; Heltec tails 2.100;
microphone and amplifier 2.600; R8 2.800. Lid clearances: Heltec header to clamp
2.800, OLED supplement 3.699, amplifier 4.800. Intended PCB support regions leave
0.0232 at the bottom soldermask and 0.0100 at the top silkscreen in the imported
model. These are support/contact regions, not general component clearance targets.
Locating-lip clearance is 0.300 per side; plunger guide clearance is 0.300 radial.
The shown plunger has a 0.200 release gap above the switch.

PTT stop revision

The baseline had no defined stop for normal switch depression. This revision
adds a separate PTTStopCup beneath the lid and two matching lid fastener holes.
The original base and plunger geometry are retained. There are now four printed
parts: base, modified lid, original plunger, and stop cup.

The cup is centred at carrier X51,Y9: outside diameter 16.6, cavity diameter
13.2, and contact-foot bore diameter 5.2. Its 2.0-thick stop shelf spans Z20.15
to Z22.15; the cup extends to the lid underside at Z24. Two diameter-6 mounting
lugs are centred at (42,9) and (60,9). Diameter-2.4 holes pass through cup and lid.

Parameter B59 sets nominal switch depression to 0.25. From the shown plunger
pose, 0.20 closes the release gap and another 0.25 reaches the cup stop: 0.45
downward motion total. With the plunger raised to its upper retention position,
free motion before switch contact is 0.40. The stop transfers further downward
load through the plunger flange, cup, fasteners and lid.

The 0.25 setting uses comparable 6 x 6 switches with 8 mm actuator height:
BBJ's drawing gives a diameter-3.5 actuator, height 8 +/-0.2 and travel
0.25 +/-0.1 [1]. Same Sky TS02-66-80 lists 0.25 nominal travel [2]. These are
comparators, not the identified specification or safe-overtravel rating of the
owned switch. No physical switch measurements were required for this revision.

To change the target, edit B59, recompute, and re-export/reprint the cup. A 0.10
shim on the stop shelf reduces nominal depression from 0.25 to 0.15. Before use,
gently check electrical continuity on press and reliable release; the nominal
stop setting does not establish that the actual switch actuates or tolerates it.

Hardware, printing and assembly

Use two M2 x 10 pan-head screws to DIN 7985 / ISO 7045, installed from outside
the lid, and two M2 full nuts inside. The reviewed screw envelope is diameter-4
head, maximum head height 1.72 [3]; nuts are 4 across flats and 1.6 thick [4].
These dimensions are the fastener fit basis, not a required supplier or material.

Print the cup with its flat bottom on the bed and open cavity facing up. Use
ORIGINAL-DESIGN-NOTES.txt orientations for the other parts: base floor down;
lid outside face down; plunger with appropriate support for its projecting foot
and flange. Deburr holes and sliding surfaces; check the slicer preview.

1. Insert the plunger through the lid from inside, leaving its flange inside.
2. Place the cup over the contact foot and flange, with its cavity facing the lid.
3. Install the two screws from outside and the nuts underneath the cup lugs.
   Tighten gently while keeping the plunger free to slide.
4. Fit the PCB and route the leads using the original enclosure assembly notes;
   close the case and perform the gentle continuity/release check above.

Package and verification scope

Delivered revision files: LoRa-Enclosure-C5-Fit-Checked.FCStd and matching .step;
enclosure-base.stl, enclosure-lid.stl, ptt-plunger.stl and ptt-stop-cup.stl.
All four native parts are valid, closed single solids. The saved STEP contains
exactly four matching solids with zero symmetric-difference volume against the
FCStd geometry. Recomputing the saved document preserves that geometry. The
cup and modified lid match the independently audited geometry exactly.
All four STL files passed topology checks: one connected closed component each,
no boundary/nonmanifold edges, no inconsistent winding, and no degenerate facets.
These checks do not establish printer accuracy or material strength.
The cup has at least 4.234 mm clearance to modeled electronics. At 0.45 downward
plunger movement, flange-to-stop contact is 91.860 square mm with zero overlap;
another 0.01 produces overlap, confirming the modeled stop. Hardware envelopes
also clear the electronics. B59 changes to 0.15/0.35 were checked independently;
the live parametric rebuild was checked at 0.35 then restored to 0.25.
Final evidence: final-cad-export-validation.json, mesh-validation.json,
native-shape-validation.json, saved-document-xml-audit.json, and
provisional-ptt-stop-cup-audit.json. The FCStd includes the actual reference PCB
and component models in mounted positions. The STEP/STLs contain printed parts
only. This revision's PTT instructions supersede the original design notes.
The imported models include open surfaces derived from VRML. Physical tolerances,
USB cable boots/insertion, wire routing and purchased inserts remain unmodelled.
