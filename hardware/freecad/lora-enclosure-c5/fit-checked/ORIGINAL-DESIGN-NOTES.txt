LoRa walkie-talkie C5 enclosure - parametric prototype
Prepared 2026-11-24. Dimensions are millimetres.

Scope and status
The user confirmed USB power and an external speaker. This enclosure follows the
current Heltec V4 audio carrier C5 project, rather than the older generic ESP32
bench harness. It has no battery cavity or internal speaker cradle. Firmware and
PCB designs are not changed by this enclosure work.

The design contains three printable FreeCAD parts: EnclosureBase, EnclosureLid,
and PTTPlunger. The design intent is native FreeCAD features linked to the
parameter table. Edit the labelled values in column B and recompute before
exporting. The parameter CSV is a companion reference; the saved FreeCAD
document is the editable model.

This is a fit-check prototype. These notes do not assert completed CAD validation,
print validation, physical fit, PTT travel, sealing, acoustic performance, or RF
performance. Check the separately produced verification results for CAD status.
Physical checks below remain necessary even if CAD checks pass.

Coordinates and principal dimensions
Carrier coordinates use the 70 x 90 PCB lower-left corner as X0,Y0; USB points
toward +Y. The enclosure outer lower-left origin is 4.4 mm below/left of that PCB
origin: add 4.4 to carrier X and Y to obtain enclosure coordinates. Z0 is the
outside base bottom. PCB bottom Z6.4, PCB top Z8, and the lid seam Z24.

Enclosure outer size: 78.8 x 98.8 x 27.6; the PTT protrudes a further 1.8, giving
29.4 maximum height including the button. The design uses 2.4 walls, 2.4 floor,
4 high PCB supports, 1.6 PCB thickness, 16 headroom above PCB top, and a 3.6 lid.
Outside vertical corner radius is 4.5. The lid registration lip is 1.6 deep and
1.2 thick, with 0.3 clearance per side. These are prototype printing allowances,
not measured printer compensation values.

Carrier mounting axes are (4,4), (66,4), (4,86), and (66,86). The PCB design
specifies 3.2 NPTH holes with 7 diameter hardware clearance. The base bosses and
lid contact columns use 7 outer diameter at the PCB; 9 diameter tapered feet,
1.5 high, reinforce the base below the PCB. Lid columns contact only the intended
mounting clearances. Check nearby solder and components in the actual assembly.

Openings and controls (positions below remain carrier XY)
- OLED: 16.4 x 27 aperture centred at (23,58.05). The reference display window
  is 11.4 x 22, so this aperture includes an allowance for the recessed view.
  The bracket and glass geometry are manufacturer references and approximations;
  confirm sight lines and prevent the lid or any window film from loading glass.
- Microphone: 3 diameter opening at (54.5,30.5), matching the current upward-facing
  INMP441 configuration. Verify the owned module's acoustic inlet position. An
  optional soft seal or acoustic tube requires measured dimensions and testing;
  no seal or acoustic isolation is validated by this model.
- PTT: centred at (51,9); 8 diameter sliding shaft in an 8.6 hole, with a captured
  12 diameter flange and 4 diameter contact foot. The nominal foot lower face is
  Z16.2: 0.2 above the nominal 8 high switch on PCB top Z8. The top protrudes 1.8
  above the lid. The switch's actual travel, return force, friction, release gap,
  and the plunger's hard stop are not validated. Check full release and avoid
  transmitting excessive force into the tactile switch or PCB.
- USB: 16 wide x 10 high opening in the +Y wall, centred on X23, from Z11.5 to
  Z21.5. Manufacturer STEP bounds put the connector at carrier X17.330..28.670,
  Y82.803..90.400, and enclosure Z13.859..17.744. Its mouth is about 4.0 behind
  the outside wall face. Confirm full insertion/removal with the actual cable,
  including the overmould and insertion sweep; opening size is adjustable.
- RST and PRG/USER: 3.2 tool-access holes at (15.09427,85.99995) and
  (30.90578,85.99995), respectively. These positions come from S1 and S2 in the
  manufacturer STEP; the source schematic identifies S1 RESET and S2 USER.
  Button tops are nominally Z17.079. Use a suitable nonconductive tool and verify
  access to the actuators without loading neighbouring parts.
- External speaker: 5 diameter split passage at the lid seam Z24 in the right
  wall, at Y66. Two 3 x 1.2 through-wall tie slots are at Y66 +/- 5.5, Z19.5,
  for an approximately 2.5 wide cable tie. Fit the tie so it provides strain
  relief without crushing insulation; verify the actual tie thickness and leads.
- Antenna coax: 4 diameter split passage at the lid seam in the +Y USB wall,
  at X43. This is an adjustable cable route, not a specified bulkhead connector
  mount. Confirm connector passage, bend radius, antenna placement and strain
  relief against the actual coax. Keep the Heltec side FPC antenna region clear.

Mounting hardware and assembly
Use four M3 x 25 socket-head screws without countersinking and four short M3
heat-set inserts, subject to checking the actual chosen inserts. The prototype
insert pilots are 4.6 diameter and 4 deep. These dimensions are assumptions;
use the insert vendor's recommended pilot, installation method and clearance.
Do not substitute an unmeasured insert solely because its thread is M3.

The insert pocket runs from support top Z6.4 to Z2.4. A narrow blind screw-tip
relief continues to Z1.2, leaving 1.2 of floor. With an uncounterbored head seated
at lid Z27.6, a nominal 25 long screw reaches Z2.6, leaving nominal 1.4 clearance
to that relief bottom and about 3.8 insertion into a 4 high insert. Actual screw
length, washers, insert seating and thread lead-ins alter this stack. Verify the
screw neither bottoms out nor protrudes before applying clamp load. Shorter or
longer screws require recalculating this stack.

The 7 diameter boss around a 4.6 pilot has 1.2 radial plastic. Its tapered foot
reinforces the base while retaining the PCB's 7 diameter clearance at the board.
Test insert installation on a coupon in the chosen print material. Install and
cool inserts before placing electronics in the enclosure. Do not overtighten or
use the screws to force mismatched parts into alignment.

Fit the carrier on the four supports; route the USB, coax, and external speaker
leads; place the PTT plunger under the lid with the flange captured inside; fit
the lid lip; then tighten the four screws gently and evenly. Verify the lid's
columns sit on the mounting areas and do not bow the PCB. Speaker leads connect
directly to amplifier SPK+ and SPK-; neither speaker terminal connects to GND.
The project calls for paired insulated leads and no carrier speaker connector.
The removable lid gives service access to the electronics and test pads.

Printing and finishing
Base: place its outside floor on the bed. Lid: flip 180 degrees so its outside
top face rests on the bed and the lip/contact columns point upward. Review all
overhangs and openings in the slicer using the selected material and settings.

Plunger: its contact foot protrudes below the wide flange, so the flange cannot
simply lie flat on the bed. Printing with the foot down needs support beneath
the flange; printing on its side also needs an appropriate support strategy.
Inspect the sliced support, contact foot, shaft roundness, flange and hard-stop
surfaces. Remove support carefully and verify free sliding and reliable return.
The three parts are not claimed to be support-free.

Deburr cable passages and protect wires from abrasion. Fit a small sample or
draft print before committing to a finished enclosure. Verify any coating,
window film, foam, or microphone seal does not change fit or obstruct access.

Inputs that remain unmeasured or provisional
- Owned Heltec PCB/header assembly gap, solder joints, header tails, underside
  components, OLED bracket, screws, actual FPC antenna and coax routing.
- USB cable overmould, connector insertion/removal envelope, and cable flex.
- Owned microphone thickness, acoustic inlet offset and optional seal geometry.
- Amplifier height, terminals and terminal overhang, plus speaker lead diameter.
- External speaker outer dimensions and mounting pattern: deliberately no fitted
  speaker cradle is derived from missing measurements.
- Actual tactile switch height, contact position, travel and return force; PTT
  free play, required hard stop and maximum safe force.
- Actual M3 insert outside geometry, pilot requirements, usable thread depth,
  screw tolerances, and printing shrinkage/clearance.

Reference checks only: the model's Heltec header tails project approximately
1.90 below the PCB underside, leaving about 2.10 above the inside floor with
these supports. The visual OLED screw-head peak is approximately 12.279 above
PCB top, leaving about 3.72 under the lid with 16 headroom. These are model-based
allowances, not measurements of the assembled hardware.

No ingress-protection rating, weather sealing, drop resistance, acoustic tuning,
thermal limit or antenna/RF performance is established. The project firmware's
PTT enablement and audio/radio behavior remain separate software work.
